## 1. Proposed insertion to §0: model, carrier and physical-emergence non-transfer lock

## 3. Proposed insertion to §6: `RRF-CND-01` — full-configuration coincidence admissibility

**Status:** `[DEF/HEUR] [scope: proposed representation/admissibility interface; not a new primordial axiom or selected physics]`.

**Four nonidentical predicates** must remain separate: (a) equality of selected node coordinates, (b) zero edge relation, (c) exchange-fixed **complete** state, and (d) physical merge/reduction to fewer nodes. None of (a) or (b) implies (c) without a declared realization; (c) does not supply a merge map.

**[THM — standard fixed-set lemma, proof one line; not owned by RRF]** For an invariant complete state domain `\mathscr X`, exchange action `\sigma`, and deterministic `F_phys` satisfying `F_phys σ = σ F_phys` as a **fixed-model** symmetry, 

\[
 F_{\rm phys}(\operatorname{Fix}\sigma)\subseteq\operatorname{Fix}\sigma.
\]

No independent coincidence axiom follows. A parameter-family covariance law is not necessarily the same as symmetry of a fixed parameterized member. Complete state includes all transition-relevant incidence, pole/role, connection, parameter and admissible history information. `[import: RTG_ONTOLOGICAL_FOUNDATIONS_v0_9 §§3–4, §8, RF-O09-03; RTG_RRF_RESPONSE_TO_OFN_COINCIDENCE_v0_2 §§1,4]`

**Owner-facing identity–carrier–clock contract** (required for a *physical-exclusion claim*, not a requirement that every abstract model use nodes):

| Interface | Owner must name | Verification condition |
|---|---|---|
| **Identity / Action** | complete `\mathscr X`, involution `\sigma_{BC}`, its actual fixed configurations, fixed-model symmetry, permitted state/parameter sector | `F_phys σ=σ F_phys` for the selected clock, and witnessed `x∈Fixσ` physically admissible |
| **Carrier / Encoding** | `\mathcal C`, action `R`, encoding `E:\mathscr X→\mathcal C`, chosen coincidence set `\mathcal D⊂\mathcal C` | `Eσ=RE`, and an explicitly witnessed counterexample initial carrier point belongs to `E(Fixσ)`, not merely to `\mathcal D` |
| **Clock / Model** | physical `F_phys`, test map `K`, transition domain, primitive vs six-step clock, projection cadence | `EF_phys=KE` on relevant admissible states, with matched action/model conventions |
| **Merge (separate)** | quotient/reduction `M` and lower-cardinality update, if merge is claimed | a separate commuting/reduction law; cannot be deduced from equality or exchange symmetry |

For the **named conditional** raw additive projected three-edge map,

\[
\mathcal C=\{(a,b,c)\in(\mathbb R^2)^3:a+b+c=0\},\quad
\mathcal N=P_{\mathcal C}T^{\times3}|_{\mathcal C},\quad
K_6=\mathcal N^6,
\]

projection is applied *after every primitive update*. With `R(a,b,c)=(-c,-b,-a)` and `\mathcal D_{BC}=\{(a,0,-a)\}`, the conditional counterexample is

\[
 b^{(1)}=\begin{pmatrix}0\\-\frac23(\cosh\rho-1)\end{pmatrix},\qquad
 b^{(6)}=\frac1{36}\begin{pmatrix}
 3\rho^4+\eta^4\\
 \rho^4+12\rho^3\eta-12\rho^2\eta^2+12\rho\eta^3-2\eta^4
\end{pmatrix}+O(\|a\|^5).
\]

Second- and third-degree terms of `b^(6)` vanish. The first quartic component obeys `(3ρ⁴+η⁴)/36≥(ρ²+η²)²/48`, so analyticity gives failure for all sufficiently small nonzero directions; **no certified numeric radius** is supplied. The calculation establishes the noninvariance of the **specific** standard carrier coincidence embedding at both named clocks. **It does not itself prove that every such carrier point is a physically realizable complete exchange-fixed state.** `[import: RTG_ER2B_CONSOLIDATED_STUDY_v0_2 conditional map definition; RTG_RRF_RESPONSE_TO_OFN_COINCIDENCE_v0_2 §2; ER2B_COINCIDENCE_SIXSTEP_AUDIT_v0_1.zip; research-level artifact]`

A passive recharting cannot repair this failure. Reimposing linear coincidence or a new projection in the new coordinates defines a different conditional model. `\mathcal N^6` and `P_\mathcal C\circ(T^6)^{\times3}` are not silently interchangeable.
