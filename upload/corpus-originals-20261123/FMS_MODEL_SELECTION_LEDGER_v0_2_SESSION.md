## 4. NEW — cubic obstruction to curved autonomous closure

**[SESSION/ALG proof, owner review pending]** Let `T` be the isolated DYAD analytic primitive map, `T(0)=0`, `DT(0)=A`, `A^6=I`, with nonzero six-step resonant cubic `μ z|z|²` in each independent complex edge. On three independent edges `D=T^{×3}`, the six-step map is

`D⁶(w) = w + μ (w₁|w₁|²,w₂|w₂|²,w₃|w₃|²) + O(|w|⁴)`.

Let `M` be an invariant local `C³` real codimension-2 manifold through the origin. Its tangent `P=T₀M` is invariant under `A⊕A⊕A`. Because `A` has nonreal conjugate eigenvalues, every real `A⊕A⊕A`-invariant subspace is invariant under the induced complex structure `J=(A−cos(π/3)I)/sin(π/3)`. Thus a real codimension-2 tangent `P` **must** be a complex hyperplane `P={a₁w₁+a₂w₂+a₃w₃=0}`, and is necessarily tangent-invariant under the cubic field:

`Σ a_e w_e|w_e|² = 0 for every w ∈ P`.

**Proof of cubic tangency:** Writing `M` as the graph of `C²+C³+o(|w|³)` over `P`, and restricting the six-step map `id+X₃+O(4)` to it, the leading normal displacement is its normal projection of `X₃|P`. The curvature of `M` contributes only at order ≥4 to this normal displacement, as `D⁶−id=O(3)`. Since `μ≠0`, the displayed condition follows.

**Classification of invariant complex hyperplanes.**

- Only one nonzero `a_k`: `P={w_k=0}`; invariant under diagonal cubic.
- Exactly two nonzero coefficients: `P={w_i=cw_j}` for a complex constant `c`. Requiring cubic invariance gives `c|c|²=c`, so `|c|=1` (and `c≠0`).
- Three nonzero coefficients: choose `w₂=0` and `w₁=0` in turn. Invariance forces `|a₁/a₃|=|a₂/a₃|=1`. Now set `w₁=a₃/a₁`, `w₂=a₃/a₂`, `w₃=−2`. The linear relation holds, but the cubic expression is `a₃+a₃−8a₃=−6a₃≠0`. Contradiction.

**Conclusion:** No local invariant `C³` codimension-2 closure manifold can be tangent at the origin to a *genuine three-edge complex-linear relation* (all `a_e≠0`) under three autonomous identical primitive DYAD maps. This is an exact local differential-geometric obstruction under the stated linear action and chart assumptions, not a physical no-holonomy theorem.

**Important limitation:** 'Any curved closure' means any `C³` closure manifold with such a three-edge `A^{×3}`-invariant tangent hyperplane. It does not exclude other tangent types, enlarged carriers, singular/non-submersive constraints, less regular invariants, dynamically coupled edge laws, or network-level geometries with different closure operations. Invariance of `w_i=cw_j` in the **full primitive map** is not established by the cubic classification; it requires additional nonlinear checks. `c=−1` does not by itself prove physical coincidence.
