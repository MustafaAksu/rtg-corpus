## §12. Emergent Relational Ontology `[ONTO legacy / OPEN]`

`[DEF]` This section records conceptual legacy material developed around the L0→L1 and NS/spark interpretation. It is an ontology-design layer, not a result layer. It may guide new probes, but it does not override current RTG Ontological Foundations, which state that isolated nodes have no physical state and that physical content is relational.

### §12.1 Primitive Carrier State

`[ONTO legacy]` **Primitive carrier hypothesis.** A node-like item before full relation may be modeled as a minimal dynamical carrier

$$
N_i=(\omega_i,\phi_i,A_i),
$$

where $\omega_i$ is intrinsic motion tendency, $\phi_i$ is phase state, and $A_i$ is optional amplitude/strength.

`[OPEN]` **Relational-primacy compatibility.** Determine whether the reduced carrier

$$
N_i=(\omega_i,\phi_i)
$$

can be used without reintroducing isolated-node ontology. The DSN-safe interpretation is: $N_i$ is a bookkeeping carrier whose physical content appears only through relational comparison.

`[DEF]` Candidate relational differences:

$$
\Delta\omega_{ij}=\omega_i-\omega_j,
\qquad
\Delta\phi_{ij}=\phi_i-\phi_j.
$$

### §12.2 Recognition Primitive

`[ONTO legacy]` **Recognition as primitive event.** The first interaction is not an external force and not a quantity computed from pre-existing relational structure. It is a *detection event*:

$$
\mathcal{R}_{AB}: \quad A \text{ detects } B \text{ as different}.
$$

This event is logically prior to any interaction law. Before $\mathcal{R}_{AB}$ occurs, $A$ and $B$ are independent carriers. After $\mathcal{R}_{AB}$, a relation space exists between them. The event is the bridge from isolated carrier to relational object.

`[DEF]` Candidate functional form of the recognition event:

$$
\mathcal{R}_{AB} = g(\Delta\omega_{AB}, \Delta\phi_{AB}),
$$

where the simplest candidate is:

$$
\mathcal{R}_{ij} := \Delta\omega_{ij} = \omega_i - \omega_j.
$$

Candidate relational motion difference (rate-of-phase version):

$$
\Omega_{ij} := \dot\phi_i - \dot\phi_j.
$$

`[OPEN]` Determine whether $\mathcal{R}_{AB}$ can be formalized as a threshold-crossing or topological event (not merely a continuous quantity), and whether either $\mathcal{R}_{ij}$ or $\Omega_{ij}$ can reproduce any known L0/L1, Dyadic, or TDR-G interaction trigger after proper bridge/revalidation.

`[OPEN]` **OPEN-L0L1-O2 connection.** See OPEN registry for programme-level routing to OPEN-P0 and OPEN-OFN-4.

### §12.3 Emergent Third Object / Manifold Hypothesis

`[ONTO legacy]` **Spark as possible relation-birth.** The QR/NS-like spark boundary may mark not only loss of stability but the emergence of a new dynamical object:

$$
A+B\rightarrow C.
$$

Here $C=F(A,B)$ is **not** assumed to be $A+B$, a coupling parameter, or a simple linear combination of parent states. $C$ is a **new degree of freedom** with its own independent phase and frequency:

$$
\omega_C \neq \omega_A,\; \omega_C \neq \omega_B, \qquad \phi_C \neq \phi_A,\; \phi_C \neq \phi_B.
$$

The child object $C$ may be a new mode, invariant manifold, quasi-invariant structure, phase-locking relation, or covariance channel — but whichever form it takes, it is an actor, not a descriptor. Once formed, $C$ can participate in subsequent dynamics on equal footing with $A$ and $B$. This is what distinguishes emergence from ordinary coupling.

`[OPEN]` Required signatures for $C$ as a genuine new degree of freedom:

- new frequency peak distinct from $\omega_A$ and $\omega_B$;
- new phase-locking relation not reducible to parent phases;
- new Lyapunov direction in the tangent space;
- new covariance channel in the LOO decomposition;
- persistent post-spark structure not expressible as a function of parent variables alone.

`[DEF]` The current dynamical-probe alternatives in §3.4 are therefore extended: fixed point, basin collapse, limit cycle, irregular regime, or emergent-mode/manifold formation.

### §12.4 Recursive Feedback and Circular Causality

`[ONTO legacy]` **Emergent feedback hypothesis.** If a new mode $C$ forms, it may become an active participant in the next state rather than a passive diagnostic:

$$
(A,B,C)\rightarrow(A',B',C').
$$

A schematic dynamical form is:

$$
\dot A=f(A,B,C),
\qquad
\dot B=f(B,A,C),
\qquad
\dot C=f(C,A,B).
$$

`[ONTO legacy]` **Circular causality.** The deeper structural consequence is not merely feedback but recursive state-space closure: the created thing modifies its creators. Schematically:

$$
C = f(A, B), \qquad A' = F(A, C), \qquad B' = F(B, C),
$$

which creates the recursive loop:

$$
(A, B) \;\longrightarrow\; C \;\longrightarrow\; (A, B).
$$

This is not a vicious circle in the logical sense — it is a causal structure where the emergence of $C$ expands the state space from $(A,B)$ to $(A,B,C)$, and subsequent dynamics operate in the larger space. The new manifold is not external; it becomes part of the next-generation dynamics.

`[OPEN]` Test whether post-spark behavior that appears as secondary oscillation, irregularity, or strange-window behavior is better explained by feedback from an emergent mode $C$ than by simple fixed-point loss or basin escape. Specific prediction: if circular causality is operative, removing $C$ from the state description should not reproduce the irregular behavior.

### §12.5 Derived Status of $K$, $dt$, and $\gamma$

`[ONTO legacy]` **Effective-parameter hypothesis.** The operational parameters $K$, $dt$, and $\gamma$ should not be treated as primitive ontology in this legacy module.

Candidate derived interaction intensity:

$$
K=f(\Delta\omega,\Delta\phi,A_i,A_j).
$$

`[ONTO legacy]` **Candidate derivations.** Each parameter has a specific candidate interpretation:

| Parameter | DSN-safe interpretation | Candidate derivation |
|---|---|---|
| $K$ | effective coupling / interaction intensity | $K = f(\Delta\omega, \Delta\phi, A_i, A_j)$; external $K$ is a reduced representation of relational mismatch |
| $dt$ | observation or update resolution; not physical external time | discretization of transition resolution; not a primitive |
| $\gamma$ | damping/relaxation scale; effective parameter pending derivation | $\gamma = -\log|\lambda|$ where $\lambda$ is the stability eigenvalue; emerges from the contraction rate of the dynamics |

`[ONTO legacy]` **Proto-time / causal ordering.** Physical time $t$ and the update step $dt$ are not primitive. Instead, the model may require only a causal ordering:

$$
n = 0, 1, 2, \ldots
$$

with state transitions:

$$
X_{n+1} = F(X_n),
$$

where $n$ is a causal index, not a physical time variable. Under this interpretation, $dt$ is the measurement or update resolution chosen by the observer, not a fundamental constant. This removes $dt$ and $\gamma$ from the ontological primitive list and relocates them to the observer/measurement layer.

`[OPEN]` Derive, reject, or axiomatize the status of $K$, $dt$, and $\gamma$ within the L0→L1 model family. Specifically: (1) express $\gamma$ as $-\log|\lambda|$ and verify against the plateau Lyapunov value; (2) construct $K = f(\Delta\omega, \Delta\phi)$ for the ringdiag geometry and test whether it reproduces the QR spark bracket; (3) restate the model using causal index $n$ and verify that $dt$-independence holds for plateau-regime observables.

### §12.6 NS/Spark as Birth of Relation

`[ONTO legacy]` **Relation-birth interpretation.** Before spark, two carriers may behave as independently rotating structures:

$$
A+B.
$$

At resonance/spark, a relation or mode may appear:

$$
A\leftrightarrow B,
\qquad
C=(\omega_C,\phi_C).
$$

Possible frequency routing:

$$
\omega_C\approx \omega_A+\omega_B
\quad\text{or}\quad
\omega_C\approx |\omega_A-\omega_B|,
$$

depending on interaction symmetry.

`[OPEN]` This interpretation is admissible only if supported by dynamical-probe signatures: frequency creation, phase-locking, Lyapunov-direction change, or covariance-channel birth. It must not be stated as a Neimark-Sacker theorem or as a certified RTG birth mechanism without revalidation.

### §12.7 Parent-Child-Triad Model and Minimal Closed Loop

`[ONTO legacy]` **Parent-child emergence.** The $A+B\to C$ ontology, with $C$ as a new degree of freedom, produces a minimal three-body system:

$$
(A, B, C), \qquad C = A \otimes B,
$$

where $\otimes$ denotes some relational combination (not necessarily tensor product — the notation is schematic for "C arises from the relation between A and B").

`[ONTO legacy]` **Triad as minimal closed feedback structure.** The dyad $A \leftrightarrow B$ is a directed relation but not a closed loop. The triad:

$$
A \leftrightarrow B \leftrightarrow C \leftrightarrow A
$$

forms the minimal closed relational loop. This is not an arbitrary geometric claim — it follows from the $A+B\to C$ emergence hypothesis: once $C$ exists as an actor and participates in the recursive dynamics $(A,B,C)\to(A',B',C')$, the three-body mutual interaction constitutes a closed feedback structure that the two-body system cannot produce.

`[ONTO legacy]` **Connection to current triadic studies.** This legacy ontology is a precursor to, but not equivalent to, the current continuous $\Phi_{2\to3}$ birth theorem and the $K_3 / K_4$ lift programme. The legacy argument is at the level of design hypothesis; the current programme has certified algebraic results. The relationship between them must go through OPEN-P2A (dyad-triad primacy) and OPEN-P2B (dynamical reduction) — not through direct analogy.

`[OPEN]` **OPEN-L0L1-O6 — Triad closure from emergence.** Can the minimal-closed-loop argument be made rigorous? Specifically: does the $(A,B,C)$ recursive feedback structure necessarily produce closed-loop topology, and is that topology equivalent to $K_3$ or a different relational complex?  
Connection: OPEN-P2A / triad primacy; OPEN-P2B / triadic reduction.

### §12.8 Tetrahedral Selection Question

`[ONTO legacy]` **Tetrahedral structure as minimum 3D embedding.** If the triad $K_3$ is the minimal closed relational loop (§11.7), the next question is why the tetrahedral structure $K_4$ appears. One candidate argument from the legacy programme:

- Two mutually constrained nodes define a relation (dyad).
- Three mutually constrained nodes define a closed relational loop (triad).
- Four mutually constrained nodes form the minimum structure in which three independent closed loops are simultaneously satisfiable and in which a 3D embedding is non-degenerate.

Under this argument, the tetrahedron is not geometrically imposed but is the minimum complete relational complex for 3D capacity.

`[OPEN]` **OPEN-L0L1-O7 — Tetrahedral natural selection from relational closure.** Can the selection of $K_4 /$ tetrahedral structure be derived from relational closure requirements (minimum 3D embedding, all-pairs mutual constraint, or holonomy completeness) rather than imposed geometrically? This question is related to but not equivalent to OPEN-GEN-TETH-SELECT in the Genesis Geometry DSN.  
Connection: Genesis DSN OPEN-GEN-TETH-SELECT; OPEN-P2A / triad primacy; Ontological Foundations OPEN-OFN-3C.
