## 1. N-3A — local triviality of the dynamically induced eta-winding cocycle

### Statement

Let
\[
\widetilde M=\mathbb R\times\mathbb R,
\qquad
p(\rho,\widetilde\eta)=(\rho,\widetilde\eta\bmod 2\pi),
\]
and choose the natural lift
\[
\widetilde T(\rho,\widetilde\eta)
=
\bigl(\rho-\sin\widetilde\eta,\;
\widetilde\eta+e^\rho-1-\sin\widetilde\eta\bigr).
\]
The deck group is generated by
\[
\tau_k(\rho,\widetilde\eta)
=(\rho,\widetilde\eta+2\pi k),\qquad k\in\mathbb Z.
\]
Then
\[
\widetilde T\circ\tau_k=\tau_k\circ\widetilde T
\]
exactly.

There exists a sufficiently small punctured N-1 base sector `U0` and its six-phase lifted tube
\[
\widetilde{\mathcal U}
=
\bigcup_{j=0}^{5}\widetilde T^{\,j}(\widetilde U_0)
\]
such that

1. `p` is injective on `\widetilde{\mathcal U}`;
2. `\widetilde T(\widetilde{\mathcal U})\subseteq\widetilde{\mathcal U}`;
3. `\widetilde{\mathcal U}\subset\{|\widetilde\eta|<\pi/2\}`.

Consequently the canonical deck index of every finite primitive history on the corresponding projected tube `\mathcal U=p(\widetilde{\mathcal U})` is zero. Hence the dynamically induced monoid winding map is trivial, and its extension to the N-2 group completion is
\[
\rho_{\rm wind}:G_T\to\operatorname{Deck}(\widetilde M/M),
\qquad
\rho_{\rm wind}(h)=\tau_0
\]
for all `h`. Thus
\[
\ker\rho_{\rm wind}=G_T.
\]

### Proof

The commutation identity follows from periodicity:
\[
\sin(\widetilde\eta+2\pi k)=\sin\widetilde\eta.
\]
Therefore
\[
\widetilde T(\rho,\widetilde\eta+2\pi k)
=
\tau_k\widetilde T(\rho,\widetilde\eta).
\]

For the local tube, L1 gives a sufficiently small punctured base sector on which `F=T^6` contracts `Q_true` and therefore maps a sufficiently small `Q_true`-ball into itself. Because each finite iterate `\widetilde T^j`, `j=0,\ldots,6`, is continuous at the fixed point and sends the fixed point to itself, shrink the base radius once more so that
\[
\widetilde T^j(\widetilde U_0)\subset\{|\widetilde\eta|<\pi/2\},
\qquad j=0,\ldots,6.
\]
The same shrinking, together with L1 and the fact that the natural lifted six-step map is continuous and fixes the origin, selects the zero-deck branch of `\widetilde T^6` and gives
\[
\widetilde T^6(\widetilde U_0)\subseteq\widetilde U_0.
\]
Hence the six-phase union is forward invariant under `\widetilde T`.

Since the whole lifted tube lies in a strip of width less than `2\pi`, projection is injective there and supplies a single chart/trivialization. For any projected state `x\in\mathcal U`, let `\widetilde x` be its unique lift in `\widetilde{\mathcal U}`. Forward invariance gives
\[
\widetilde T^r\widetilde x\in\widetilde{\mathcal U}
\]
for every finite `r`. The lifted endpoint therefore always remains in the same chosen deck sheet. Its deck index relative to the chart is exactly zero.

Thus the actual orbit cocycle sends every forward history `T^r` to `\tau_0`. The Grothendieck extension satisfies
\[
\rho_{\rm wind}([T^r,T^s])
=
\tau_{0-0}=\tau_0.
\]
QED.

### Guard

This is **not** a theorem that every bounded orbit or every bounded six-cycle has zero winding. The statement is local to a sufficiently small one-chart N-1 tube around the elliptic fixed point. Abstract nontrivial homomorphisms `G_T\cong\mathbb Z -> Deck\cong\mathbb Z` exist, but they are not dynamically induced by the local primitive eta history.

## 2. N-3B — abstract character representation

Let
\[
\widehat{S^1}
=
\{\chi_k:k\in\mathbb Z\},
\qquad
\chi_k(z)=z^k,
\]
under pointwise multiplication. Then
\[
\chi_k\chi_m=\chi_{k+m},
\qquad
\chi_0=1,
\]
and `\widehat{S^1}` is infinite cyclic with distinguished generator `\chi_1`.

Let `g=[T,id]` be the distinguished generator of the N-2 signed-history group `G_T`. Define
\[
\rho_{\rm char}:G_T\to\widehat{S^1},
\qquad
\rho_{\rm char}(g^k)=\chi_k.
\]
This is the unique group homomorphism sending `g` to `\chi_1`. It is surjective because every character is `\chi_k`, and its kernel is trivial because `\chi_k=\chi_0` as functions on `S^1` only for `k=0`. Therefore
\[
G_T\overset{\sim}{\longrightarrow}\widehat{S^1}.
\]

**Epistemic status:** standard algebra applied to the already-constructed RTG group. It is a faithful abstract representation, not a theorem that primitive dyadic evolution increments character labels.

## 3. N-3C — exact primitive pullback and non-translational character dynamics

For the phase character
\[
\chi_m(\eta)=e^{im\eta},
\]
the exact primitive pullback is
\[
\boxed{
T^*\chi_m(\rho,\eta)
=
\chi_m(T(\rho,\eta))
=
e^{im(e^\rho-1)}\,e^{-im\sin\eta}\,\chi_m(\eta).
}
\]

For any `m\ne0`, this cannot equal `\chi_{m+c}(\eta)` for any fixed integer `c` on an open dyadic domain, because the left-hand side has nontrivial `\rho` dependence whereas a pure circle character has none. Hence primitive `T` does **not** act as a constant translation of harmonic labels.

Using the standard Jacobi-Anger expansion,
\[
e^{-im\sin\eta}
=
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}J_n(-m)e^{in\eta},
\]
so
\[
\boxed{
T^*\chi_m
=
e^{im(e^\rho-1)}
\sum_{n\in\mathbb Z}J_n(-m)\,\chi_{m+n}(\eta).
}
\]
Thus the primitive dynamics mixes phase-character sectors (with `\rho`-dependent amplitudes) rather than shifting the character exponent by one fixed history unit.

**Interpretation:** N-3B is faithful algebraically; N-3C shows that this faithful abstract representation is not the primitive dyadic Koopman action on phase characters.

## 4. N-3D — finite-character quotient

For any integer `N>=1`, let
\[
C_N=\{e^{2\pi i j/N}:j=0,\ldots,N-1\}\subset S^1,
\]
canonically identified with a cyclic phase group after a generator is chosen. Restriction of continuous characters gives
\[
\operatorname{res}_N:\widehat{S^1}\to\widehat{C_N},
\qquad
\operatorname{res}_N(\chi_k)=\psi_{[k]_N},
\]
where
\[
\psi_{[k]_N}(e^{2\pi i j/N})=e^{2\pi i kj/N}.
\]
This map is surjective and
\[
\ker\operatorname{res}_N
=
\{\chi_{qN}:q\in\mathbb Z\}.
\]
Composing with N-3B,
\[
\rho_N
:=
\operatorname{res}_N\circ\rho_{\rm char}:G_T\to\widehat{C_N},
\]
we obtain
\[
\rho_N(g^k)=\psi_{[k]_N},
\qquad
\ker\rho_N=N\,G_T,
\]
and therefore
\[
G_T/(N\,G_T)\cong\widehat{C_N}\cong\mathbb Z_N.
\]

For the TDR-G bridge application, `N` is the already-specified even phase size of the discrete branch. This theorem transfers **only representation structure**. It transfers no primitive map, coefficient, threshold, normal-form datum, or dynamical law between branches.
