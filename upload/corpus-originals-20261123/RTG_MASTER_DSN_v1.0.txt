  KAM theory applies to Hamiltonian (conservative) systems.
  This Kuramoto system with damping γ is dissipative.
  Do not claim KAM stabilization without conservative justification or
  Fenichel NHIM extension. Use "observed protective modulation" language.

  Extrapolation beyond this range is untested.
  Sinusoidal coupling bounds Im(σ), so true exponential at large φ requires
  specific amplification mechanism. Do not extrapolate freely.

  Do not equate n_active_faces=2 with geometric boundary.
  Bottleneck hinges are (k-core≥2)∧(n_active=2) and are typically embedded.

  Adjacency lift is operational at current resolution; not a continuum claim.

  This confound is structural in the bottleneck definition (all bottlenecks
  have n_active=2 by definition).
  Do not attribute κ₃^dens differences to relational localization without
  null model controlling for n_active.

  Always: face_max := min(requested, audit_faces_total).

  Does NOT prove mobility, global landscape non-glassiness, or
  dynamical lability under ω,φ evolution.
