## 1. What is accepted

The C1–C4 spine is sound, SG-NG-25's prefix-cofinal reformulation is the right correction (event-count divergence as directed unboundedness under prefix extension, not completed infinity), and the C6 falsifier stands. C5-SYN-13 is correctly and explicitly fenced as an abstract control.

N5-PHYS-1's proof is also **valid as stated**. I reproduced it: `B_+` and `B_-` strictly alternate on the dyad with `|Δ_{1:1}| ≤ 1` at every checkpoint. The gauge audit is right too — a half-turn in the derived angle is not an admissible identification of the two transition neighbourhoods.

## 2. The problem: the witness does not discriminate

I reran the identical construction on a **control system** — a pure order-6 linear rotation with mild contraction, which has no slips, no `{6,7}` alphabet, no logarithmic defect, and none of the LOGSLIP structure that N-5A-S1 worked to certify.

| class | RTG dyad | pure-rotation control |
|---|---|---|
| `Δ_{1:1}` (`B_+` vs `B_-`) | `[0,−1,0,0]` | `[0,−1,0,−1]` |
| `Δ_{2:1}` (`B_+∪B_-` vs `C_+`) | `[0,−1,0,−2]` | `[−2,−1,−2,−1]` |
| `Δ_{4:1}` (all four rays vs `C_+`) | `[0,−2,0,−3]` | `[−3,−2,−3,−2]` |
| `Δ_{3:2}` (`B_+,B_-,C_+` vs `C_+,C_-`) | `[0,1,0,−1]` | `[−1,1,−1,1]` |

The control reproduces every bounded class identically. **So `1 ∈ Q_RTG^real` is certified but carries no information about the RTG dyad**: it would hold for any monotone lift whatsoever.

**Mechanism.** A monotone lift crosses each fixed ray exactly once per lap. For ray-families of sizes `m₁, m₂`, the counts therefore satisfy `N₁/N₂ → m₁/m₂` with bounded defect *by construction*. The bounded class is fixed by **how many rays the predicate names**, not by the dynamics. This is the section-level analogue of the concern SG-NG-20 already raises about fixed re-blocking of a single stream.

## 3. The sharper consequence: C7B can close positively but spuriously

Here `C_±` denotes crossings of `ρ = 0` (amplitude coincidence) with `η_wrap ≷ 0`. These are relationally clean by **exactly** the v1.9 argument that admitted `η_wrap = 0` — each is the zero set of one relational coordinate with a sign condition on the other — so they are admissible on the same footing as `B_±`. In `Θ` coordinates the four rays sit at `0, 2π/3, π, 5π/3`, all members of the canonical six-fold family generated by the certified order-6 linearization.

Consequently `2:1`, `4:1` and `3:2` bounded classes are **already realizable in the continuous branch today**, with no pending artifact and no firewall crossing. A naive reading would close C7B immediately with `{1, 2, 3/2, 4, …} ⊆ Q_RTG^real`. Row A5 shows why that would be an error: the control realizes them too.

This matters because it is a *positive* result that looks like success. C6 was designed to catch a spurious negative; nothing currently in the module catches a spurious positive.

## 4. Proposed remedy

**`SG-NG-26` — section-multiplicity triviality (proposed).**
> If two event streams are both crossing-counts of finite fixed ray-families of one monotone lift on a common domain, their synchronization defect is bounded with reduced class equal to the ratio of ray multiplicities, identically, for every monotone lift including the linearization. Such pairs certify that the C1–C4 operator functions correctly, but do **not** populate `Q_RTG^real` with dynamically contentful elements.

**Discrimination-witness requirement (proposed).** Every claimed element of `Q_RTG^real` must be accompanied by an explicit admissible comparison dynamics on the same domain under which the same two predicates fail to yield the same reduced class. Without such a witness the element is recorded as a *positive control*, alongside C5-SYN-13, rather than as a physical realization.

**Re-tagging suggestion.** Under that criterion N5-PHYS-1 should be relabelled from "the first genuine RTG positive realization" to **the first physical positive control** — it demonstrates the operator fires on genuinely distinct physical predicates, which is real progress, but not that `1` is a dynamically realized rational.

## 5. This strengthens the C7B-DISC-1 routing

The instinct to go to native-discrete TDR-G is correct, and this audit explains *why* it is necessary rather than merely preferable: SWAP and active-P3 events are not two ray-families of one monotone lift, so a bounded non-`1` class there would not be a multiplicity artifact.
