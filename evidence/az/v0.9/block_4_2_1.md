#### §4.2.1 AZ-owned diagnostic — seed-token resonance and tail risk (Action 1, Stage 1)

`[NUM diagnostic]` `[RID: AZ-NUM-TOKRES-S1]` `[scope: pinned TDR-G engine with TDRG-DEF-ORIENT; policy bound_symmetric; $N\in\{120,122\}$, $k=8$; four initial nodes; 800 ticks; node cap 300; endpoint $M\ge100$]` `[conv: orientation reference of TDR-G v2.12.14 (profile P1)]`
*Owned by this DSN. It is computed on the TDR-G engine, which is not changed; it is not a TDR-G result, not a bridge, and no bridge-candidate is promoted by it.*

**Statement.** In a registered, paired intervention on the engine's own initial draw — one of the four initial tokens $\delta_j$ is replaced, and the same base draw is run under every arm — replacing the token by a value $x$ with $4x\equiv0\pmod N$ raises the probability that the run ends with at least 100 nodes, against replacing it by a neighbour $x\pm1$. This holds in the three contrasts in which such a value is set (PC1, PC2, PC3). PC4 is a different kind of contrast, an equivalence control: at $N\equiv2\pmod 4$, where $2x\equiv N/2$ has no solution, the values nearest $N/4$ are compared with the native state and show no effect.

| Contrast (1,500 base draws each) | Resonant arm | Comparison arm | Risk difference, 97.5 % Agresti–Min paired-proportion interval | Discordant pairs | Registered reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PC1 — $2x\equiv0$, $N=120$ | 0.2580 | 0.0153 (detuned) | $+0.2427$ $[+0.2166, +0.2681]$ | 374 / 10 | supported; Holm $p=1.780\cdot10^{-96}$ |
| PC2 — $2x\equiv N/2$, $N=120$ | 0.1853 | 0.0240 (detuned) | $+0.1613$ $[+0.1381, +0.1841]$ | 259 / 17 | supported; Holm $p=9.375\cdot10^{-57}$ |
| PC3 — $2x\equiv0$, $N=122$ | 0.2307 | 0.0113 (detuned) | $+0.2193$ $[+0.1944, +0.2436]$ | 336 / 7 | supported; Bonferroni-adjusted $p=2.375\cdot10^{-89}$ |
| PC4 — values nearest $N/4$, $N=122$ | 0.0087 | 0.0160 (native) | $-0.0073$ $[-0.0158, +0.0011]$ | 10 / 21 | equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.05$ |

PC1 and PC2 are the confirmatory family (Holm over the two). PC3 and PC4 are a separate family at $N\equiv2\pmod 4$ and are supportive only (Bonferroni over the two). Tests are exact McNemar tests on the paired outcomes. The registered controls read as required: at $N=120$ the node-label permutation left every compared quantity unchanged (0 of 400 permuted runs differ); in both cells a $\pm1$ perturbation of a non-resonant native token and the detuned replacement are equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.025$ (95 % Agresti–Min intervals) on the draws whose native slot token is non-resonant; and the exact per-tick token law of the registration had no violation in any of the 19,900 runs. All four readings carry the registered qualifier "unqualified": every registered control of the cell read as required. The qualifier is about the controls; it is not an additional, independent validation.

**What the statement is.** The total causal effect of the token replacement on the registered endpoint, for the engine, profile, cells, horizon, cap and threshold named in the scope.

**What it is not.** No mechanism is established. The free parent–child phase law of the registration is a conditional kinematic identity under a token-persistence hypothesis — a `[SESSION]` statement offered to TDR-G, not a result of this DSN — and its proposed role in the selection of the tail basin is untested. Nothing is shown about other $N$, other $k/N$, other policies, longer horizons or other thresholds; the endpoint is a threshold on the node count $M$, an integer-valued outcome whose distribution is not separated into two groups at the threshold, and it is not the TDR-26 birth-anchored discriminator. Nothing is stated about TDR-26 or about any open module of TDR-G. No relation is established or claimed to the Hashimoto `4|q` rule of §4.3.3, to Dyadic cyclic-subgroup selection or to any DZT object; the shared numeral is not evidence of one (SG-AZ-3, SG-AZ-7). OPEN-P1 is untouched (SG-PROG-1). By the PI's outcome rule of 12 November 2026 the diagnostic does not by itself close `AZ-OPEN-PROMO`, change the maturity level, or become a TDR-G claim. The algebraic statements of the registration (the signed token alphabet and its corollaries) are offered to TDR-G through `pipes/TDRG/` and are not results of this DSN.
