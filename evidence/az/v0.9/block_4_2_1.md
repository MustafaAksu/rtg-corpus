#### §4.2.1 AZ-owned diagnostic — seed-token resonance and tail risk (Action 1, Stage 1)

`[NUM diagnostic]` `[RID: AZ-NUM-TOKRES-S1]` `[scope: pinned TDR-G engine with TDRG-DEF-ORIENT; policy bound_symmetric; $N\in\{120,122\}$, $k=8$; four initial nodes; 800 ticks; node cap 300; endpoint $M\ge100$]` `[conv: orientation reference of TDR-G v2.12.14 (profile P1), the edition pinned when the runs were made; TDRG-DEF-ORIENT is unchanged at tdrg-v2.12.15]`
*Owned by this DSN. It is computed on the TDR-G engine, which is not changed; it is not a TDR-G result, not a bridge, and no bridge-candidate is promoted by it.*

**Statement.** In a registered, paired intervention on the engine's own initial draw — one of the four initial tokens $\delta_j$ is replaced, and the same base draw is run under every arm — replacing the token by a value $x$ with $4x\equiv0\pmod N$ raises the probability that the run ends with at least 100 nodes, against replacing it by a neighbour $x\pm1$. This holds in the three contrasts in which such a value is set (PC1, PC2, PC3). PC4 is a different kind of contrast, an equivalence control: at $N\equiv2\pmod 4$, where $2x\equiv N/2$ has no solution, the values nearest $N/4$ are compared with the native state and show no effect.

| Contrast (1,500 base draws each) | Resonant arm | Comparison arm | Risk difference, 97.5 % Agresti–Min paired-proportion interval | Discordant pairs | Registered reading |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| PC1 — $2x\equiv0$, $N=120$ | 0.2580 | 0.0153 (detuned) | $+0.2427$ $[+0.2166, +0.2681]$ | 374 / 10 | supported; Holm $p=1.780\cdot10^{-96}$ |
| PC2 — $2x\equiv N/2$, $N=120$ | 0.1853 | 0.0240 (detuned) | $+0.1613$ $[+0.1381, +0.1841]$ | 259 / 17 | supported; Holm $p=9.375\cdot10^{-57}$ |
| PC3 — $2x\equiv0$, $N=122$ | 0.2307 | 0.0113 (detuned) | $+0.2193$ $[+0.1944, +0.2436]$ | 336 / 7 | supported; Bonferroni-adjusted $p=2.375\cdot10^{-89}$ |
| PC4 — values nearest $N/4$, $N=122$ | 0.0087 | 0.0160 (native) | $-0.0073$ $[-0.0158, +0.0011]$ | 10 / 21 | equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.05$ |

PC1 and PC2 are the confirmatory family (Holm over the two). PC3 and PC4 are a separate family at $N\equiv2\pmod 4$ and are supportive only (Bonferroni over the two). Tests are exact McNemar tests on the paired outcomes. The registered controls read as required: at $N=120$ the node-label permutation left every compared quantity unchanged (0 of 400 permuted runs differ); in both cells a $\pm1$ perturbation of a non-resonant native token and the detuned replacement are equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.025$ (95 % Agresti–Min intervals) on the draws whose native slot token is non-resonant; and the exact per-tick token law of the registration had no violation in any of the 19,900 runs (the law is since `tdrg-v2.12.15` an owner result of TDR-G in the owner's formulation, `TDRG-ALG-TOKEN-CENSUS-LAW`, §8; what was checked is the registration's formulation). All four readings carry the registered qualifier "unqualified": every registered control of the cell read as required. The qualifier is about the controls; it is not an additional, independent validation.

**What the statement is.** The total causal effect of the token replacement on the registered endpoint, for the engine, profile, cells, horizon, cap and threshold named in the scope.

**What it is not.** No mechanism is established. The free parent–child phase law is a conditional kinematic identity under a token-persistence hypothesis — since `tdrg-v2.12.15` an owner result of TDR-G (`TDRG-ALG-PARENT-CHILD-PHASE` `[ALG]`, §8), which the owner states is not a growth mechanism and has no bearing on TDR-26; it is not a result of this DSN, it names the arithmetic condition of the intervention and does not explain its effect, and its proposed role in the selection of the tail basin is untested. Nothing is shown about other $N$, other $k/N$, other policies, longer horizons or other thresholds; the endpoint is a threshold on the node count $M$, an integer-valued outcome whose distribution is not separated into two groups at the threshold, and it is not the TDR-26 birth-anchored discriminator. Nothing is stated about TDR-26 or about any open module of TDR-G. No relation is established or claimed to the Hashimoto `4|q` rule of §4.3.3, to Dyadic cyclic-subgroup selection or to any DZT object; the shared numeral is not evidence of one (SG-AZ-3, SG-AZ-7). OPEN-P1 is untouched (SG-PROG-1). By the PI's outcome rule of 12 November 2026 the diagnostic does not by itself close `AZ-OPEN-PROMO`, change the maturity level, or become a TDR-G claim. The algebraic statements of the registration (the signed token alphabet and its corollaries) were offered to TDR-G, which accepted them with change and registered them as its own `[ALG]` results at `tdrg-v2.12.15` §6.13, credited to AZ (§8); they are not results of this DSN. TDR-G did not adopt this diagnostic or its numbers: `AZ-NUM-TOKRES-S1` remains an AZ-owned `[NUM diagnostic]`.
