**Statement.** In the registered paired intervention of §4.2.1, run in three further cells, replacing one initial token by a value $x$ with $2x\equiv0\pmod N$ rather than by its neighbour $x\pm1$ raises the probability of the endpoint in all three cells, and replacing it by a value with $2x\equiv N/2$ does so in the two cells where such values exist ($N=240,480$), on both registered endpoints. All ten contrasts of the single confirmatory family meet the registered support criterion for a risk difference above $\delta^*=0.05$. At $N=242$, where $2x\equiv N/2$ has no solution, the near-quarter arm — the replaced token drawn from the four values nearest $N/4$ and $3N/4$ — is equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.05$ on both endpoints.

The registered support criterion is: Holm-adjusted exact McNemar $p<0.05$ over the ten, and a lower limit of the 99.5 % interval above 0.05. The ten intervals are Bonferroni intervals with nominal 95 % joint coverage under the normal approximation; no exact finite-sample familywise coverage is claimed. A run that reached the cap of 300 nodes before tick 800 counts as positive on E_100. Near-quarter control at $N=242$, separate from the family (99.5 % interval, margin $\pm0.05$; one registered mixture arm in which the replaced token is drawn from the four values, not four separate tests): E_θ $-0.0040$ $[-0.0144, +0.0064]$, E_100 $-0.0020$ $[-0.0095, +0.0055]$, both equivalent to the native state.

The registered controls read as required in every cell and on both endpoints: node-label permutation left every compared quantity unchanged (0 of 400 permuted runs differ at $N=240$ and at $N=480$); the $\pm1$ perturbation (sham) is equivalent to the native state within $\pm0.025$ on the base draws whose native **and** perturbed slot token are both non-resonant, and each detuned replacement is equivalent to it within $\pm0.025$ on the base draws whose native slot token is non-resonant (sixteen 95 % Agresti–Min intervals in all, not adjusted for multiplicity; one excludes zero inside the margin). The equivalences are stated for those subsets only. All ten readings carry the registered qualifier "unqualified". The per-tick token law of the registration had no violation in the 26,300 campaign runs or in the pilot files read by the analysis. The thresholds $\theta(N)$ and horizons were fixed before the campaign by an exploratory native-arm calibration pilot to the reference exceedance rate of $N=120$ at 800 ticks; in the campaign's own non-resonant native draws the rate at $\theta$ is 0.0071, 0.0041, 0.0076, each point estimate inside the pilot's interval $[0.0029, 0.0090]$ (a consistency report, not an equivalence test). The two endpoints are both confirmatory by a design decision made after the pilot and before the campaign, and recorded as such.

**What the statement is.** A statistical reading of the total causal effect of the token replacement on each registered endpoint, for the engine, profile, policy, cells, horizons, cap and thresholds named in the scope. Together with §4.2.1 the contrast is observed at $N=120,122,240,242,480$.

**What it is not.** No mechanism is established, as in §4.2.1; that the arithmetic condition acts through the free parent–child phase law (`TDRG-ALG-PARENT-CHILD-PHASE`, §8) is untested. No dependence or independence of the effect on $N$ is tested or claimed, and the endpoints are not asserted to be the same event at different $N$: the thresholds $\theta(N)$ were calibrated to match approximately the reference exceedance rate in non-resonant native pilot draws, whose arithmetic composition differs with $N$; equality of the population rates is not shown, and that the campaign's out-of-sample rates lie inside the pilot's interval is not an equivalence test. Nothing is shown about other $k/N$, other policies, profiles, horizons or thresholds; the endpoints are thresholds on the integer-valued node count $M$ and are not the TDR-26 birth-anchored discriminator. Nothing is stated about TDR-26 or any open module of TDR-G; an exploratory breakdown of native draws by the additive order of $2x$ is recorded in the report and is untested. No relation is established or claimed to the Hashimoto `4|q` rule of §4.3.3, to Dyadic cyclic-subgroup selection or to any DZT object (SG-AZ-3, SG-AZ-7). OPEN-P1 is untouched (SG-PROG-1). By the PI's outcome rule of 12 November 2026 the diagnostic does not by itself close `AZ-OPEN-PROMO`, change the maturity level, or become a TDR-G claim; TDR-G receives no separate item for it (PI, 16 November 2026).
