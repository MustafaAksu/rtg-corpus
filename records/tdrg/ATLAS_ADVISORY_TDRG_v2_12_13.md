## 2. The orientation policy at S=0, |E|=N/2 — assessment (TA-01, TC-14)

**Owner text (§6.3, line 572).** Path orientation rule for P_n, n≥3: S = Σ signed_defect_error(θ_{v_i}, θ_{v_{i+1}}, N); forward if S>0, reversed if S<0; if S=0, E = signed_centered(δ_{v_n} − δ_{v_1}, N); "if S=E=0: balanced path-tie". Neither `signed_centered` nor `signed_defect_error` is defined in the DSN; the pinned implementation (`genesis_mode_b_mc_v18.py` lines 38–55, cited by `TDRG-ART-GENESIS-MC-V18` as "convention ground truth") defines `signed_centered(x) ∈ (−N/2, N/2]` and `signed_defect_error(θ_i,θ_j) = signed_centered((θ_i−θ_j) mod N − N/2)`, lists a path from its smaller-ID endpoint (`raw_ordered_path`, `sorted(endpoints)`), and at S=0 returns "forward" whenever `signed_centered(δ_last − δ_first) > 0` (`orient_path`).

**The degeneracy (NUMGEN's finding, confirmed).** Under reversal of the listing, S ↦ −S and E ↦ −E except exactly when δ_{v_n} − δ_{v_1} ≡ N/2 (mod N), where `signed_centered` returns +N/2 under **both** listings. On that set (S=0 ∧ E≡N/2) the pair (S,E) carries no reversal-odd information, and v18 orients "forward" relative to the node-ID listing: the successor shift applied depends on which endpoint has the smaller ID. That is exactly the fallback the owner itself retired — Appendix B: "[FAIL] Arbitrary path label fallback as tie-breaking — background-independence violation" — and it sits inside a certification that reports "relabeling-covariance check: PASS" (§6.12, d-resolved anatomy), where the check performed is the ±d mirror, not node permutation.

**Atlas check, N=24, three nodes, k∈{0,1,2,3} (exhaustive over the 331,776 gauge-reduced states; results `…_check.json`).**
- **C1** — of 145,152 (state,k) cases whose defect graph is a two-edge path, **1,152 are degenerate**; S is reversal-odd everywhere, E is reversal-odd everywhere except on that set, where E = +N/2 under both listings. PASS.
- **C2** — on the degenerate set the centre-relative advances are **never reversal-symmetric**: with c the centre, d_{1c} + d_{c3} = δ_{v_1} − δ_{v_3} ≡ N/2 ≠ 0, so the forward pair (d_{1c}, d_{c3}) can never equal its reversal (−d_{c3}, −d_{1c}). Concretely, G := signed_centered(δ_{v_1} − δ_c) − signed_centered(δ_{v_3} − δ_c) is reversal-odd and **never 0** on the degenerate set. PASS. Hence for P_3 a relabelling-covariant orientation exists on the whole degenerate set — there is no genuine tie there. (For P_n with n even a palindromic configuration with a middle advance ≡ N/2 can exist, so a general rule must still allow a tie; P_3 is the only path type certified by CONV-P3CYC and the only one in the three-node universe.)
- **C3** — the 136 ratified DISC-2 witnesses rerun under four policies at the degenerate set: **v18** (ID listing; 40 witnesses exposed — reproduces NUMGEN's audit), **reverse** (10 verdict/class changes — reproduces), **inert** (20 — reproduces), **covariant-G** (12). The admitted ten-label set is identical under all four; every label keeps a witness that never touches the condition.
- **C4** — census of the whole three-node universe at k=1 (the BIND3 certification universe, §6.12): under v18, **77 of 9,800 eventual cycles** contain a listing-dependent event and **16,322 of 331,776 states (4.9 %)** flow into such a cycle; under covariant-G 9,857 cycles (134 touched); under inert 9,789 cycles (66 touched). So the exact BIND3 enumeration (3,456 retrigger-entry ICs, "both P_3 conventions") was run on a universe whose dynamics depend on node IDs on a small but nonzero set; whether any of the 3,456 ICs or the 10,368 one-pass ICs is touched is not determined here (the IC construction script is session-held, `TDRG-ART-BIND3-N24K1` "consolidate into bind3_cycle_n24k1.py") — the owner can determine it with the flag in the atlas script.

**Options for the owner (scientific decision; the atlas recommends nothing beyond "covariant and declared").**
- (a) **Covariant tie-break on the degenerate set**: for P_3 orient by sign(G); for general P_n use the first reversal-odd component of the centre-relative advance sequence and declare a tie only on palindromic configurations. Changes 12 DISC-2 witness records; never produces a tie at P_3.
- (b) **Extend the balanced path-tie** to S=0 ∧ E≡N/2 (treat the degenerate value as "no orientation"): the simplest covariant rule, in the spirit of `bound_symmetric` (inert ties). Changes 20 DISC-2 witness records.
- (c) Declare node identity physical (keep v18): covariant under nothing; contradicts Appendix B's own [FAIL] and the relational premise TDR-G imports from OFN (O0–O3) — not a live option in the atlas reading.
Either (a) or (b) requires, in the same edition: the two missing definitions (TA-02); a declared relabelling-covariance guard (TA-03); a `[COR]` on the §6.12 "relabeling-covariance check" wording; a statement of which v2.12 certificates are touched (BIND3-CYCLE-N24K1 and the policy-fate claims of §6.6–6.8 run on v18 — the census bounds the exposure at 4.9 % of states at k=1; a rerun is the owner's call, not this advisory's); and a notice to NUMGEN (DISC-1/DISC-2 witness annotations; C7C-M4 HOLD release), which NUMGEN has already prepared for (v1.21 §2 item 1).
