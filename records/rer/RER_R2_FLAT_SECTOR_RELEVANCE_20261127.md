## 3. Bounded new mathematical comparisons (SESSION, not adoption)

### 3.1 Common symmetry and modal cubic

[SESSION] [ALG] In native flat C2, M(λz)=λM(z). Slot permutation and complex-linear P_C commute with the common λ action, so G and F_C commute with common multiplication by λ, in particular -I4. The same holds for C1, where the stronger common U(1) equivariance is exact. The quadratic signed-Gram readout is invariant under these actions. Therefore the full C2 successor readout is identical at X and λX, and all odd total-degree Taylor terms of quadratic readout iterates vanish. This gives the C03/C04/C05/C06 limitations in §2. It does not imply U(1)-equivariance for C2 or physical gauge; in arbitrary raw readouts the nonlinear chart can change these identities.

[OWNER: GEN v2.10 §6.3c; OFN v0.13 §5.5.4] The closure-plane six-step cubic is f_e=μ(w_e|w_e|²−mean_j w_j|w_j|²), μ=−1/2−2i√3/3. In the native smooth flat slice F_C=id+f+O(5) by oddness. Direct substitution gives the modal components
    f_s=μ(3Qz/4+d² bar(z)/3+2Cz/3),
    f_c=μ(z² bar(d)/4+Qd/2+Cd).
This is a use of the owner cubic, not a new independent derivation of its primitive-map proof.

### 3.4 Birth graph and finite age

[SESSION] [ALG] In native coordinates choose a trial C_b(z)=ibQz, b fixed real nonzero (not coupling κ). At graph states d=C_b(z)=O(|z|³), the degree-five child contribution from f_c is ib μ Q²z/4, while D C_b(z)f_s(z,0)=ib(3/4)(μ+2Re μ)Q²z. The difference is
    F_{C,c}(z,C_b(z))−C_b(F_{C,s}(z,C_b(z)))
      = b(−√3/3+i) Q² z + O(|z|⁷).
The remainder follows for the exact cubic trial: smooth odd Taylor jets, slot invariance F_{C,c}(z,0)=0, and terms of state-degree ≥5 in the child have at least one child factor, so they contribute only at degree ≥7 after the trial substitution. The parent correction beyond cubic enters D C_b at ≥7. An odd smooth O(|z|⁵) graph correction also leaves the leading defect unchanged. Thus native graph noninvariance persists but with a different coefficient. For each fixed pair of unequal finite ages the leading graph separation is (n−m) times this defect, by the graph-transform expansion and its tangent-to-identity parent map; no uniform age bound, no new physical event. ### 3.5 Formal structural reuse

[SESSION] [ALG] The existing RER formal-age construction is not tied to the raw cubic coefficients: for any formal F=id+O(3) preserving the child-zero ideal, U=F*−id raises degree by at least 2 and preserves that ideal. Inverting the formal tangent-identity parent map preserves the polynomial-degree budget (composition adds the degrees-minus-one costs for parent slots), so the graph coefficient of degree d has deg_τ≤floor((d−3)/2). This reuses the structural construction with new coefficients on the flat class-C formal jet; it does not realize convergence or make τ physical. For an odd trial and odd map all age graph jets are odd. No finite-order check implies a regional error bound.
