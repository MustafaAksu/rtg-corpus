## 1. Setting and the exact record structure

Primitive orbit $x_n=T^nx_0$, $x_0\in U$; lifted phase $\hat\varphi_n$ with principal one-step increments $\delta_T\in(\tfrac\pi6,\tfrac\pi2)$ (RECOG-THM-A1A-LIFT-ALIGNMENT); anchors $n_j$ = the $2\pi$-crossings (Lemma A), $\ell_j=\ell_0+j$, overshoot $o_j:=\hat\varphi_{n_j}-2\pi\ell_j\in[0,\delta_T(x_{n_j-1}))$; six-step deficit $\Delta_j:=2\pi-\Lambda_6(x_{n_j})\in(0.927\tau Q,\tau Q+1.82Q^{3/2})$ at $Q=Q(x_{n_j})$ (Lemma C′).

**Proposition 6 (threshold coding).** For every $j\ge0$: $g_{j+1}=7$ iff $o_j<\Delta_j$, and
$$o_{j+1}=\begin{cases}o_j-\Delta_j,&g_{j+1}=6,\\ o_j-\Delta_j+\delta_T(x_{n_j+6}),&g_{j+1}=7.\end{cases}$$
Consequently $\mathcal R(x)=\mathcal R(x')$ iff $n_0(x)=n_0(x')$ and $[o_j(x)<\Delta_j(x)]=[o_j(x')<\Delta_j(x')]$ for all $j$.
*Proof.* $\hat\varphi_{n_j+6}=2\pi\ell_j+o_j+2\pi-\Delta_j$. If $o_j\ge\Delta_j$ the lift has reached $2\pi(\ell_j+1)$ by step $n_j+6$ and not before (no 5-gap, Lemma B), so $n_{j+1}=n_j+6$ and $o_{j+1}=o_j-\Delta_j$. If $o_j<\Delta_j$, it has not; the next increment $\delta_T(x_{n_j+6})$ carries it past (no 8-gap, Lemma B), so $n_{j+1}=n_j+7$ and $o_{j+1}=o_j-\Delta_j+\delta_T(x_{n_j+6})$. The record is $(n_0;g_1g_2\ldots)$. $\square$
*Reading.* Between slips the overshoot decreases by $\Delta_j\approx\tau Q_{M_j}$ per return from $\approx\delta_T\approx\tfrac\pi3$ down to $<\Delta_j$; the record is the sequence of return indices at which the sawtooth is reset. The quantity ${\rm crit}_j:=|o_j-\Delta_j|(a+M_j)/\tau$ (margin to the threshold in units of the threshold width $\tau/(a+M_j)$) is $\ge0$, and at the slip return and the return before it lies in $(0,1)$ up to $O(\sqrt Q)$.

## 2. The asymptotic phase is $C^1$ with nonvanishing angular derivative

Chart (row A): $z=re^{i\Theta}$, $q=r^2$, $\lambda=\log q$; $\widehat F(z)=z(1+u)$, $u=\mu q+v$, $v=a_{32}q^2+a_{05}\bar z^6/q+\rho_7$, $\rho_7=R_7(z)/z$ with $|R_7|\le C_7|z|^7$, $\|DR_7\|_2\le C_D|z|^6$ (NF-REM). Block base points of starts in $U$ have $q\le q^*=\tfrac{256}{140625}$ (NF-INV). Polar step: $\lambda'=\lambda+2\operatorname{Re}\operatorname{Log}(1+u)$, $\Theta'=\Theta+\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Log}(1+u)$ ($|u|<10^{-2}$; the lift of $\Theta$ along the orbit is by these principal increments). $\Psi:=\Theta-\tau\lambda$; $\Psi_\infty(z_0):=\lim_m\Psi(z_m)$ (exists: RECOG-THM-A1A-PHASE-EXISTENCE-U; also directly from $\sigma=O(q^2)$ below).

**Lemma 10 (the log-spiral cancellation).** $\sigma(z):=\Psi(\widehat Fz)-\Psi(z)=\operatorname{Im}[(1-2i\tau)\operatorname{Log}(1+u)]$ and $(1-2i\tau)\mu=-\tfrac{19}6\in\mathbb R$. Hence, with $\bar v=A_5q^2+C_7q^3$, $\bar u=|\mu|q+\bar v$, $\bar v_\lambda=2A_5q^2+\tfrac12(C_D+C_7)q^3$, $\bar v_\Theta=6|a_{05}|q^2+(C_D+C_7)q^3$ (ceilings of $|v|,|u|,|\partial_\lambda v|,|\partial_\Theta v|$; $\partial_\lambda f=\tfrac12Df[z]$, $\partial_\Theta f=Df[iz]$, $\|D\rho_7\|\le(C_D+C_7)|z|^5$):
$$|\partial_\lambda\sigma|\le c_{\sigma\lambda}q^2,\quad|\partial_\Theta\sigma|\le c_{\sigma\Theta}q^2,\qquad c_{\sigma\lambda}=11.75,\ c_{\sigma\Theta}=5.37\quad(q\le q^*),$$
from $\partial_\bullet\sigma=\operatorname{Im}[(1-2i\tau)\partial_\bullet u/(1+u)]$, $\partial_\lambda u=\mu q+\partial_\lambda v$, $\partial_\Theta u=\partial_\Theta v$, and $\operatorname{Im}[-\tfrac{19}6q/(1+u)]=\tfrac{19}6q\operatorname{Im}u/|1+u|^2$ with $|\operatorname{Im}u|\le\tau q+\bar v$. (G1: the identity, exact; G2: the constants.) $\square$

**Lemma 11 (polar Jacobian).** $J=D(\lambda',\Theta')/D(\lambda,\Theta)=I+\begin{pmatrix}2\operatorname{Re}A&2\operatorname{Re}B\\\operatorname{Im}A&\operatorname{Im}B\end{pmatrix}$, $A=\partial_\lambda u/(1+u)$, $B=\partial_\Theta u/(1+u)$, with $J_{11}=1-q+e_{11}$, $J_{21}=-\tau q+e_{21}$, $|e_{11}|\le\alpha q^2$, $|J_{12}|\le\beta q^2$, $|e_{21}|\le\tfrac\alpha2q^2$, $|J_{22}-1|\le\tfrac\beta2q^2$, where $\alpha=2\bar e_A/q^2$, $\beta=2\bar e_B/q^2$, $\bar e_A=(|\mu|q\bar u+\bar v_\lambda)/(1-\bar u)$, $\bar e_B=\bar v_\Theta/(1-\bar u)$; on $q\le q^*$: $\alpha=9.59$, $\beta=4.27$ ($\beta$ is dominated by $(C_D+C_7)q^*$: NF-REM's derivative ceiling). $\square$

**Proposition 7.** `[ALG]` target [scope: chart row A, $0<q_0\le q^*$; conditional on DYAD-THM-NF-REM ($C_7$, $C_D$), DYAD-THM-NF-INV, DYAD-ALG-NF6] $\Psi_\infty$ is $C^1$ in $(\lambda_0,\Theta_0)$ on the punctured row disk and $|\partial_{\Theta_0}\Psi_\infty-1|\le c_\Psi q_0$ with $c_\Psi=11.13$; moreover $\partial_{\lambda_0}\Psi_\infty=-\tau+O(q_0)$.
*Proof.* $\Psi_\infty=\Psi(z_0)+\sum_{m\ge0}\sigma(z_m)$, each term $C^\infty$. Let $w_m=(p_m,t_m)=\partial_\xi(\lambda_m,\Theta_m)$, $w_{m+1}=J(z_m)w_m$. For $\xi=\Theta_0$, $w_0=(0,1)$ and Lemma 11 gives $|p_{m+1}|\le(1-q_m+\alpha q_m^2)|p_m|+\beta q_m^2|t_m|$, $|t_{m+1}|\le(\tau q_m+\tfrac\alpha2q_m^2)|p_m|+(1+\tfrac\beta2q_m^2)|t_m|$. With $q_m^{-1}=b+m+\epsilon_m$, $|\epsilon_m|\le\bar\epsilon_m$ (part 1b Lemma 2), $q_m\le\tfrac1{(1-\kappa)(b+m)}$, $S_2=\sum q_m^2\le2q_0+q_0^2$ and $\prod_{i=j+1}^{m-1}(1-q_i+\alpha q_i^2)\le e^{\alpha S_2}\tfrac{b+j+1+\bar\epsilon_m}{b+m}$ (from $\sum_{i=j+1}^{m-1}q_i\ge\log\tfrac{b+m+\bar\epsilon_m}{b+j+1+\bar\epsilon_m}$): $|p_m|\le C_pT\,\tfrac{1+\Lambda(m)}{b+m}$, $\Lambda(m)=\sum_{j<m}\tfrac1{b+j}\le q_0+\log(1+\tfrac mb)$, $T=\sup_m|t_m|$, $C_p=\tfrac{\beta e^{\alpha S_2}}{(1-\kappa)^2}\bigl[(1+\tfrac1b)+(e_{\rm const}+2\gamma_0+1)(\tfrac1{b^2}+\tfrac1b)\bigr]$. Grönwall: $T\le1+T\,\Xi$ with $\Xi=\tfrac{\tau C_p}{1-\kappa}\sum\tfrac{1+\Lambda(j)}{(b+j)^2}+\tfrac{\alpha C_p}{2(1-\kappa)^2}\sum\tfrac{1+\Lambda(j)}{(b+j)^3}+\tfrac\beta2S_2$ (closed forms $\sum_{m\ge0}\tfrac{1+\Lambda(m)}{(b+m)^p}\le(1+q_0)P_p+LG_p$, $P_p=b^{-p}+\tfrac{b^{1-p}}{p-1}$, $LG_p=2b^{-p}+\tfrac{b^{1-p}}{(p-1)^2}$); at $q_0=q^*$: $\Xi=0.0267$, $T\le1.028$. (G2 evaluates every constant in interval arithmetic at $q^*$, all terms being increasing in $q_0$.) $\square$

**Corollary 8 (fibers lie on one $C^1$ curve; empty interior).** `[ALG]` target [scope: $x_0\in U$; chart of row A; conditional as Prop. 7 plus the adopted RESULT and RECOG-THM-A1A-PHASE-EXISTENCE-ALT] For $\psi\in\mathbb R/2\pi\mathbb Z$ the set $\mathcal C_\psi=\{z_0:\Psi_\infty(z_0)\equiv\psi\}$ in the row is the graph of a $C^1$ function $\Theta_0=\theta_\psi(q_0)$, $0<q_0\le q^*$. If $\mathcal R(x)=\mathcal R(x_0)$ then $z(x)\in\mathcal C_\psi$ with $\psi=L(\mathcal R)-\tfrac\pi3n_0$. Hence the record fiber has empty interior, is parametrized injectively by $q_0$ (equivalently by the chart amplitude, and by $a$ on the sampled curves), and the record determines the start up to one real parameter.
*Proof.* $\Psi_\infty(q_0,\Theta_0+2\pi)=\Psi_\infty(q_0,\Theta_0)+2\pi$ and $\partial_{\Theta_0}\Psi_\infty\ge1-c_\Psi q^*>0$: for fixed $q_0$, $\Theta_0\mapsto\Psi_\infty$ is a $C^1$ increasing bijection of one period onto one period, so each class $\psi$ has exactly one preimage $\theta_\psi(q_0)$ mod $2\pi$, and $\theta_\psi\in C^1$ by the implicit function theorem. The record determines $L=\tfrac\pi3n_0-2\pi\ell_0+\Psi_{W,\infty}$ (RESULT), $n_0$ is part of the record, $\ell_0\in\mathbb Z$, and $\Psi_{z,\infty}=\Psi_{W,\infty}$; so $\Psi_\infty(z(x))\equiv L-\tfrac\pi3n_0\pmod{2\pi}$. The chart $x\mapsto z$ is a diffeomorphism onto its image (NF-INV). $\square$

**2.4 Asymptotics along $\mathcal C_\psi$ (`[SESSION/HEUR]`; the first identity is exact, every order statement below it is heuristic and uncertified).** From the finite-block identity, $\hat\varphi_{6M}=2\pi M+\Psi_{W,\infty}-\tau\psi(a+M)+\tau S^{\rm tail}_M-R^{\rm tail}_M$; for $x,x'\in\mathcal C_\psi$ with parameters $a,a'$ the first two terms cancel and $\hat\varphi_{6M}-\hat\varphi'_{6M}=-\tau[\psi(a+M)-\psi(a'+M)]+\tau\Delta S^{\rm tail}_M-\Delta R^{\rm tail}_M$. The chart angles satisfy $\Theta_m-\Theta'_m\to\tau\log(q_m/q'_m)\approx\tau\log\tfrac{b'+m}{b+m}=O(\Delta a/m)$ (same $\Psi_\infty$), so the oscillatory tails differ by $O(\Delta a\,M^{-3/2})$ (part 1b, Lemma 5 with $\bar\eta$ replaced by the angle difference), and $\hat\varphi_{6M}-\hat\varphi'_{6M}=-\tau\Delta a/M\,(1+o(1))$. The thresholds differ by the same relative order. Since $\Delta_j\approx\tau/(a+M_j)$, return $j$ flips for the partner iff $|\Delta a|>{\rm crit}_j$ on the matching side (Prop. 9 below), and Q1′ reduces to the accumulation of the margins at $0$.

**Proposition 9 (reduction; exact).** For $x,x'\in\mathcal C_\psi$: $\mathcal R(x)=\mathcal R(x')$ iff $n_0=n_0'$ and, for every $j$, $[o_j<\Delta_j]=[o'_j<\Delta'_j]$. Injectivity of $\mathcal R$ on $U$ is equivalent to: for every $\psi$ and every pair of distinct points of $\mathcal C_\psi$ some return has different coding. $\square$ (Prop. 6 restricted to the curve; Cor. 8 for the reduction to one curve.) The margin criterion ${\rm crit}_j<|\Delta a|$ of §2.4 is **not** part of this proposition: it rests on the uncertified orders of §2.4 and is `[SESSION/HEUR]`.
*What this does and does not give.* A proof of injectivity needs a lower bound on how often the sawtooth lands close to the threshold (an equidistribution statement for the slip margins along every orbit); a counterexample needs an orbit whose margins stay bounded away from $0$ on one side forever. Neither is available from the adopted statements, and neither can be certified by a finite computation; the question is declared OPEN in this sharpened form. What *is* established: the fiber is a subset of a $C^1$ curve (Cor. 8); non-injectivity would mean distinct starts on one curve with identical infinite records — possibly a segment, possibly a discrete set such as two points; nothing here distinguishes these. The `[SESSION/HEUR]` one-sided margin bounds on the fiber's $a$-extent (§0 item 4) are expectations consistent with the `[NUM]` pairs, not results.
