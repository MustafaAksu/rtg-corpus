## 1. Lemma 16 (first-anchor sector)

Let $x_0\in U$, $\hat\varphi_0=\theta_{W,0}=\operatorname{Arg}W_0\in(-\pi,\pi]$, and $n_0\ge1$ the first anchor. By Lemma A, $\hat\varphi_{n_0}\in[2\pi\ell_0,2\pi\ell_0+\delta_T(x_{n_0-1}))$ for the integer $\ell_0$. With $\hat\varphi_{n_0}=\theta_{W,0}+\sum_{i<n_0}\delta_T(x_i)$ and $|\delta_T(x_i)-\tfrac\pi3|\le\bar\alpha_0:=\arcsin\bar\varepsilon(Q^\ddagger)$, $Q^\ddagger:=f^{\circ n_0}(Q_0)$, $f(Q)=Q(1+\bar\varepsilon(Q))^2$ (amplitude ceiling on $x_0,\ldots,x_{n_0}$; $Q_0=1/a$ known):
$$\theta_{W,0}\in\bigl[2\pi\ell_0-n_0\tfrac\pi3-n_0\bar\alpha_0,\ 2\pi\ell_0-(n_0-1)\tfrac\pi3+(n_0+1)\bar\alpha_0\bigr),$$
and modulo $2\pi$ the integer $\ell_0$ drops out. The chart angle satisfies $\Theta_0=\theta_{W,0}-D_\Theta(z_0)$, $|D_\Theta(z_0)|\le D_0^+:=\sup|d|\sqrt{q_0^+}+c_1q_0^+$, $q_0^+=\tfrac{256}{225a}$ (NF-INV; $\sup|d|=0.2955$, $c_1=0.261$). $\square$ Widths at $a=625$: $\tfrac\pi3+(2n_0+1)\cdot0.0282$ ($n_0=6$: $1.41$); $D_0^+=0.013$. G2: the sector contains the true $\theta_{W,0}$ in 12/12 (classes $n_0=2,3,5,6$); NC1: the sector built with $n_0+1$ excludes it for some orbit.

## 2. Lemma 17 (signed start offset)

$a-b=Q_0^{-1}-q_0^{-1}=(q_0-Q_0)/(q_0Q_0)=-D_Q(z_0)/(q_0Q_0)$ (exact, $Q=q+D_Q$). Write $D_Q=q_0^{3/2}\Phi(\Theta_0)+\delta$, $|\delta|\le c'_Qq_0^2$ (D5), $\Phi=D_3/q^{3/2}=\operatorname{Re}(\phi_1e^{i\Theta}+\phi_3e^{3i\Theta})$ (re-derived from $h_2$, G1). Then $a-b=-\Phi(\Theta_0)\sqrt{q_0}\,a-\delta a/q_0$ with $|\delta a/q_0|\le c'_Qq_0/Q_0\le c'_Q\tfrac{256}{225}=0.420$, and $\sqrt{q_0}\,a=\sqrt a\sqrt{q_0/Q_0}$, $q_0/Q_0=1-D_Q/Q_0\in[1-x^+,1+x^+]$, $x^+=c_Qq_0^{3/2}/Q_0\le c_Q(\tfrac{256}{225})^{3/2}a^{-1/2}$, so $|\sqrt{q_0}\,a-\sqrt a|\le\sqrt a\,x^+/(2\sqrt{1-x^+})$. Hence $a-b=-\Phi(\Theta_0)\sqrt a+\epsilon_b$, $|\epsilon_b|\le\beta(a)$ as in §0 ($\sup|\Phi|=0.7734$; $\beta(625)=0.7993$, $\beta(2500)=0.7956$, $\beta(10^6)=0.7921$). For $\Theta_0\in T=[t_1,t_2]$: $\min_T\Phi,\max_T\Phi$ by a $4000$-point grid with the Lipschitz constant $|\phi_1|+3|\phi_3|=1.3819$. $\square$

## 5. Consequences and next steps

- **The start offset is no longer an $O(\sqrt a)$ unknown:** given $a$ and the record, $a-b$ is pinned to $\pm0.9$. The known-$a$ envelope is now $E^{(4)}\approx[\tau\cdot0.9+c_P(r)+c_{\rm other}]/(a+M)+\tau A_1$; for $r\le2$ slips $\approx3/(a+M)$, for $r\ge3$ the in-block residual ($\approx15/(a+M)$) dominates and is the next (small) lever (the $\|g'\|\,s\bar\alpha$ bookkeeping in $c_P$ is crude). The logarithmic $\tau A_1$ term would need the chart reciprocal drift centred (its mean coefficient is the exact $-\tfrac{29}{216}$, part 1b Lemma 2 — a candidate for a further centring).
- **Unknown $a$ (part 2):** the same centring applies inside the box algorithm once $L$ is treated jointly (2-D boxes in $(a,L)$ or an $a$-box with the current $L$-window); expected: the $a$-windows drop from $\sim2\Delta_a$ to the in-block/$A_1$ scale ($\lesssim10$). Not done here.
- **DSN candidate:** Lemmas 16–17 and Prop. 18 as `[ALG]` rows under the A1c RESULT ("the record and $a$ determine the start angle to $O(w_L)$ and the start offset to $O(1)$"), and the sharpened envelope. Not adopted.
