## 0. Summary

1. **Result (Prop. 1).** Written in the normalized chart $z=h^{-1}(W)$, $q=|z|^2$, $Q=q+D_Q(z)$, the tail of the THM obeys, for every $x_0\in U$ with $a=Q_0^{-1}$ and every $M\ge1$,
$$|S^{\rm tail}_M|\le\bar S'(a,M),\qquad \bar S'(a,M)\sqrt{a+M}\ \to\ \frac{|\phi_1|}{|\tfrac12+i\tau|}+\frac{|\phi_3|}{|\tfrac12+3i\tau|}=0.74850\quad(M\to\infty),$$
with $\phi_1=-\tfrac56-i\tfrac{\sqrt3}6$, $\phi_3=\tfrac1{12}+i\tfrac{\sqrt3}{12}$ the two angular harmonics of $D_3=2\operatorname{Re}(\bar zh_2)$, and $\bar S'$ an explicit closed-form function of $(a,M)$ (§2). Against part 1's $\bar S\approx150/\sqrt{a+M}$ this is a factor $68$ at $M=10^3$ and $\to200$. The order $M^{-1/2}$ is **not** removed — as the review stated, $\sum_{m\ge M}m^{-3/2-ik\tau}\sim M^{-1/2-ik\tau}/(\tfrac12+ik\tau)$ — the averaging step contributes exactly the factor $|\tfrac12+ik\tau|^{-1}/2$ ($0.40$ for $k=1$, $0.14$ for $k=3$) against the absolute sum; the bulk of the gain comes from the right variable (coefficient $|\phi_1|=0.88$ instead of part 1's $2\times37.5$). At leading order the bound is attained when the harmonics align, so within this decomposition it is essentially optimal.
2. **Same machinery, two more terms.** (Prop. 2) The Dyadic angular remainder has the same harmonic structure, $r_m=\Lambda_3(z_m)+O(q_m^2)$ with $\Lambda_3=L_\Theta+\tau D_3$ (harmonics $\lambda_1,\lambda_3$); hence $|R^{\rm tail}_M|\le\bar R'(a,M)$, $\bar R'\sqrt{a+M}\to1.0753$ (part 1: $9.12$). ## 2. Lemmas and propositions

**Lemma 1 (start offset).** $|a-b|\le\Delta_a:=\tfrac{16}{15}c_Q\sqrt a$; set $b':=a-\Delta_a\le b$, $\bar q_0:=1/b'\ge q_0$.
*Proof.* $a-b=(q_0-Q_0)/(q_0Q_0)=-D_Q(z_0)/(q_0Q_0)$, $|D_Q(z_0)|\le c_Qq_0^{3/2}$, $\sqrt{q_0}\le\tfrac{16}{15}\sqrt{Q_0}$ (D3). $\square$

**Lemma 2 (reciprocal law in the chart).** For $|z|^2=q\le q^*$ and $q'=|\widehat F(z)|^2$:
$$\bigl|q'^{-1}-q^{-1}-1\bigr|\le\gamma_0q+\gamma_1q^2,\qquad\gamma_0=0.17716,\ \gamma_1=205.15 .$$
Hence $q_m^{-1}=b+m+\epsilon_m$ with $|\epsilon_m|\le\bar\epsilon_m:=\gamma_0\sigma_1(m)+\gamma_1\sigma_2$, $\sigma_1(m)=\bar q_0+2\log(1+\tfrac m{2b'})$, $\sigma_2=2\bar q_0+\bar q_0^2$; with $\kappa:=\tfrac{\gamma_0\bar q_0+\gamma_1\sigma_2}{b'}+\tfrac{\gamma_0}{\sqrt2\,b'}\ (\le1.4\times10^{-3})$, $\bar\epsilon_m\le\kappa(b'+m)$ and $q_m\le\tfrac{1}{(1-\kappa)(b'+m)}$ for all $m$.
*Proof.* $\widehat F(z)=z(1+u)$, $u=\mu q+v$, $v=a_{32}q^2+a_{05}\bar z^6/q+R_7/z$, $|v|\le A_5q^2+C_7q^3$ (D1, D2). $q'=q(1+x)$, $x=2\operatorname{Re}u+|u|^2=-q+\delta$, $\delta=\delta_2q^2+\delta_3$ with $\delta_2=|\mu|^2+2\operatorname{Re}a_{32}+2\operatorname{Re}(a_{05}e^{-6i\Theta})$ and $|\delta_3|\le2C_7q^3+2|\mu|q|v|+|v|^2$. Algebra gives the exact identity
$$q'^{-1}-q^{-1}-1=\frac{q(1-\delta_2)-\delta_3/q-\delta}{1-q+\delta},\qquad 1-|\mu|^2-2\operatorname{Re}a_{32}=-\tfrac{29}{216},$$
so $|1-\delta_2|\le\tfrac{29}{216}+2|a_{05}|=0.1768$; the three terms are increasing in $q$ and are evaluated at $q^*$ in interval arithmetic (G2). The $\epsilon$ bound sums the increments with $q_j\le1/(b+j/2)$ (D1): $\sum_{j<m}q_j\le q_0+2\log(1+\tfrac{m-1}{2b})\le\sigma_1(m)$, $\sum_{j<m}q_j^2\le\sigma_2$. $\kappa$: $\sigma_1(m)/(b'+m)\le\bar q_0/b'+2\sup_m\log(1+\tfrac m{2b'})/(b'+m)$ and $\log(1+y)\le\sqrt y$, $\sqrt m/(b'+m)\le1/(2\sqrt{b'})$. $\square$
*Remark.* The mean coefficient of the $O(q)$ term is the exact rational $-\tfrac{29}{216}$ plus the sixth harmonic $-2\operatorname{Re}(a_{05}e^{-6i\Theta})$ (amplitude $0.043$); the $O(q^2)$ ceiling $\gamma_1$ is dominated by $2C_7$ and contributes only the constant $\gamma_1\sigma_2\approx0.75$ to $\bar\epsilon$. Consequently $|\epsilon_m|\le0.36\log(1+\tfrac m{2b'})+0.75$ — the chart reciprocal is linear in $m$ up to a slowly growing offset, whereas $Q_m^{-1}=q_m^{-1}-\Phi(\Theta_m)q_m^{-1/2}+O(1)$ carries the genuine $\sqrt m$ oscillation (review A1C-K2).

**Lemma 3 (phase law in the chart).** $|\Theta_{m+1}-\Theta_m+\tau q_m|\le c_\theta q_m^2$, $c_\theta=2.0766$. For $m\ge M$, exactly
$$e^{ik\Theta_m}=e^{ik\Theta_M}\Bigl(\frac{b+m}{b+M}\Bigr)^{-ik\tau}e^{ik\eta_m},\qquad\eta_m:=\sum_{j=M}^{m-1}\Bigl[\Theta_{j+1}-\Theta_j+\tau\log\frac{b+j+1}{b+j}\Bigr],\qquad|\eta_m|\le\bar\eta_M:=\frac{c_\theta P_2}{(1-\kappa)^2}+\tau\Bigl(\frac{P_2}2+\frac{\Lambda_2}{1-\kappa}\Bigr),$$
where $\Lambda_p:=\sum_{m\ge M}\bar\epsilon_m(b'+m)^{-p}=(\gamma_0\bar q_0+\gamma_1\sigma_2)P_p+2\gamma_0L_p$.
*Proof.* $\operatorname{Arg}(1+u)=\operatorname{Im}\operatorname{Log}(1+u)$, $\operatorname{Im}u=-\tau q+\operatorname{Im}v$, $|\operatorname{Log}(1+u)-u|\le|u|^2/(2(1-|u|))$; so $|\Delta\Theta+\tau q|\le|v|+|u|^2/(2(1-|u|))\le c_\theta q^2$ (evaluated at $q^*$). The representation is the telescoped definition. For $\bar\eta$: $\Delta\Theta_j+\tau\log\tfrac{b+j+1}{b+j}=[\Delta\Theta_j+\tau q_j]+\tau[\log(1+\tfrac1{n})-\tfrac1n]+\tau[\tfrac1n-\tfrac1{n+\epsilon_j}]$, $n=b+j$; $|\log(1+\tfrac1n)-\tfrac1n|\le\tfrac1{2n^2}$; $|\tfrac1n-\tfrac1{n+\epsilon}|\le\tfrac{\bar\epsilon_j}{n(n-\bar\epsilon_j)}\le\tfrac{\bar\epsilon_j}{(1-\kappa)n^2}$; $b\ge b'$. $\square$

**Lemma 4 (model sum).** For $s=\tfrac32+ik\tau$ and $n>0$: $\bigl|\sum_{m\ge0}(n+m)^{-s}\bigr|\le\dfrac{n^{-1/2}}{|s-1|}+\Bigl(\dfrac12+\dfrac{|s|}3\Bigr)n^{-3/2}$, $|s-1|=\sqrt{\tfrac14+k^2\tau^2}$ ($=1.25831$ for $k=1$, $3.5$ for $k=3$).
*Proof.* First-order Euler–Maclaurin: $\sum_{m\ge0}f(m)=\int_0^\infty f+\tfrac12f(0)+\int_0^\infty(\{x\}-\tfrac12)f'(x)\,dx$ with $f(x)=(n+x)^{-s}$; $\int_0^\infty f=n^{1-s}/(s-1)$ (G1), $|f(0)|=n^{-3/2}$, $\tfrac12\int|f'|=\tfrac12|s|\cdot\tfrac23n^{-3/2}$. $\square$ (The sum is the Hurwitz zeta $\zeta(s,n)$; G3 checks $|\zeta(s_k,b+M)|$ against the bound at both ends $b\in\{b',a\}$: ratios $0.996$–$0.9993$, i.e. the bound is tight.)

**Lemma 5 (harmonic sums).** $B_k:=\sum_{m\ge M}q_m^{3/2}e^{ik\Theta_m}$ obeys
$$|B_k|\le\bar B_k:=\frac{(b'+M)^{-1/2}}{|s_k-1|}+\Bigl(\frac12+\frac{|s_k|}3\Bigr)(b'+M)^{-3/2}+\frac32\,\frac{\Lambda_{5/2}}{(1-\kappa)^{5/2}}+k\,\bar\eta_M\,\frac{P_{3/2}}{(1-\kappa)^{3/2}} .$$
*Proof.* By Lemma 3, $B_k=e^{ik\Theta_M}(b+M)^{ik\tau}\sum_{m\ge M}q_m^{3/2}(b+m)^{-ik\tau}e^{ik\eta_m}$. Write $q_m^{3/2}e^{ik\eta_m}=(b+m)^{-3/2}+[q_m^{3/2}-(b+m)^{-3/2}]+q_m^{3/2}[e^{ik\eta_m}-1]$. First sum: Lemma 4 with $n=b+M\ge b'+M$ (both terms decrease in $n$). Second: $q_m=(b+m+\epsilon_m)^{-1}$ and $|(n+\epsilon)^{-3/2}-n^{-3/2}|\le\tfrac32|\epsilon|(n-|\epsilon|)^{-5/2}\le\tfrac32\bar\epsilon_m(1-\kappa)^{-5/2}(b'+m)^{-5/2}$. Third: $|e^{i\theta}-1|\le|\theta|\le k\bar\eta_M$ and $q_m^{3/2}\le(1-\kappa)^{-3/2}(b'+m)^{-3/2}$. $\square$

**Proposition 1 (tail of the amplitude series).** `[ALG]` target [scope: as RECOG-THM-A1B-SLIP-SCHEDULE-U; $M\ge1$] `[assuming: (D5) bridge constants $c_Q$, $c'_Q$ as published]`
$$|S^{\rm tail}_M|\le\bar S'(a,M):=A_0+A_1+|\phi_1|\bar B_1+|\phi_3|\bar B_3+\frac{c'_QP_2}{(1-\kappa)^2},\qquad A_0=\frac{\Delta_a}{(a+M)(a+M-\Delta_a)}+\log\frac{a+M}{a+M-\Delta_a},\quad A_1=\frac{\Lambda_2}{1-\kappa}.$$
*Proof.* $Q_m-\tfrac1{a+m}=\bigl[\tfrac1{b+m}-\tfrac1{a+m}\bigr]+\bigl[q_m-\tfrac1{b+m}\bigr]+D_3(z_m)+(D_Q-D_3)(z_m)$. (i) $\sum\bigl[\tfrac1{b+m}-\tfrac1{a+m}\bigr]=(a-b)\sum\tfrac1{(a+m)(b+m)}$, $|\cdot|\le\Delta_a\sum_{m\ge M}\tfrac1{(a+m)(a+m-\Delta_a)}\le A_0$ (first term plus $\int_M^\infty$, partial fractions). (ii) $|q_m-\tfrac1{b+m}|=\tfrac{|\epsilon_m|}{(b+m)(b+m+\epsilon_m)}\le\tfrac{\bar\epsilon_m}{(1-\kappa)(b'+m)^2}$, sum $\le A_1$. (iii) $\sum D_3(z_m)=\operatorname{Re}(\phi_1B_1+\phi_3B_3)$, Lemma 5. (iv) $|D_Q-D_3|\le c'_Qq_m^2$, $\sum q_m^2\le(1-\kappa)^{-2}P_2$. $\square$

**Proposition 2 (tail of the angular-remainder series).** `[ALG]` target, same scope `[assuming: (D5) remainder decomposition and constants as published]`
$$|R^{\rm tail}_M|\le\bar R'(a,M):=|\lambda_1|\bar B_1+|\lambda_3|\bar B_3+\frac{c_{r2}P_2}{(1-\kappa)^2}+\frac{c_{r3}P_3}{(1-\kappa)^3}.$$
*Proof.* $r_m=\Lambda_3(z_m)+\rho_r(z_m)$ with $\Lambda_3(z_m)=q_m^{3/2}\operatorname{Re}(\lambda_1e^{i\Theta_m}+\lambda_3e^{3i\Theta_m})$ at the same chart point; Lemma 5 and $\sum q_m^p\le(1-\kappa)^{-p}P_p$. $\square$

**Corollary 3 (block amplitude and the new envelope).** With $n_M:=b'+M-\bar\epsilon_M$: $Q_M\le\bar Q_M:=\bigl(n_M-c_Q\sqrt{n_M}\bigr)^{-1}$, hence the largest amplitude in the slip block is $\le\bar Q^*:=f^{\circ5}(\bar Q_M)$, $f(Q)=Q(1+0.705\sqrt Q+0.257Q)^2$ (part 1, Lemma 1), and the THM's error term is majorised by
$$E'(a,M)=\tau\bar Q^*+1.82\,\bar Q^{*3/2}+5\arcsin\bigl(0.705\sqrt{\bar Q^*}+0.257\bar Q^*\bigr)+\tau\bar S'(a,M)+\bar R'(a,M).$$
*Proof.* $Q_M^{-1}=q_M^{-1}(1+D_Q/q_M)^{-1}\ge q_M^{-1}(1-c_Q\sqrt{q_M})=q_M^{-1}-c_Qq_M^{-1/2}$, and $x\mapsto x-c_Q\sqrt x$ is increasing for $x>c_Q^2/4$ with $q_M^{-1}\ge b+M-\bar\epsilon_M\ge n_M$. The rest is part 1's Prop. 1 with the new majorants. $\square$ (G5: $Q_M/\bar Q_M\le0.9996$ on the sampled orbits — the bound is nearly attained.)
