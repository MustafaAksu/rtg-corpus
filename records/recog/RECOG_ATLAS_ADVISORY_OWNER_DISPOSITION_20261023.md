### FC-RECOG-21 — proposed new, unregistered science cell

`[SESSION]` With `a=Q_0^{-1}` and on the explicit primitive block base region `U={0<Q_0<=1/625}`, DYAD row A provides

\[
D_M^U=\tau\sum_{m=0}^{M-1}Q_m-\sum_{m=0}^{M-1}r_m,
\qquad |r_m|\le\frac{403}{250}Q_m^{3/2}.
\]

The existing RECOG reciprocal-amplitude theorem provides `Q_m^{-1}=a+m+O(\sqrt m)`. Thus `Q_m=1/(a+m)+O(m^{-3/2})`, while `r_m=O(m^{-3/2})`. Both discrepancies are absolutely summable, and `sum_{m<M}1/(a+m)-log M` converges. Therefore `lim(D_M^U-tau log M)` exists on every **block base point in U**, independent of R9 and INF-1. The prior finite-block identity and chart offset give the existence-only primitive and normalized phase limits. This is **not** an adoption, a claim of a full R9-free quantitative memory law, or a generator-selection theorem. Preserve the `U ∩ U_NUMGEN` NUMGEN import and its `[RESULT] [from: ALG]` classification. Verify the formula and the phase-lift branch at the adopted Dyadic tag and run a focused receiving review before integrating into stage 1.
