## 1. Lemma 21 (exact first order of the reciprocal law)

$\widehat F(z)=z(1+u)$ with $u=\mu q+a_{32}q^2+a_{05}\bar z^6/q+\rho_7$, $\rho_7=R_7/z$, $|R_7|\le C_7|z|^7$ (NF6, NF-REM); $a_{05}\bar z^6/q=a_{05}q^2e^{-6i\Theta}$. With $x:=2\operatorname{Re}u+|u|^2$, $q'=q(1+x)$ and $\tfrac1{1+x}=1-x+\tfrac{x^2}{1+x}$:
$$\frac1{q'}-\frac1q-1=-\frac{x_2}{q}+\frac{q}{1+x}-\frac{2x_2-x_2^2/q}{1+x},\qquad x=-q+x_2,\quad x_2=\bigl(2\operatorname{Re}a_{32}+2\operatorname{Re}(a_{05}e^{-6i\Theta})+|\mu|^2\bigr)q^2+{\rm br},$$
${\rm br}=2\operatorname{Re}\rho_7+2\operatorname{Re}(\bar\mu q(u-\mu q))+|u-\mu q|^2$, $|{\rm br}|\le\delta_3(q):=2C_7q^3+2|\mu|q\,v+v^2$, $v=A_5q^2+C_7q^3$. The first-order part is $(1-|\mu|^2-2\operatorname{Re}a_{32})q-2\operatorname{Re}(a_{05}e^{-6i\Theta})q$ with $1-|\mu|^2-2\operatorname{Re}a_{32}=1-\tfrac{19}{12}+\tfrac{97}{216}=-\tfrac{29}{216}$ (G1, sympy series of $1/(q(1+x))$ through order $q$). The remainder $R=-{\rm br}/q-\tfrac{qx}{1+x}-\tfrac{2x_2-x_2^2/q}{1+x}$ satisfies $|R|\le\tfrac{\delta_3}q+\tfrac{q|x|}{1-|x|}+\tfrac{2|x_2|+|x_2|^2/q}{1-|x|}$, every term$/q^2$ increasing in $q$; at $q^*=\tfrac{256}{140625}$ (block base points of starts in $U$): $\gamma_2=212.58$, of which $\delta_3/q^3\to2C_7=200$. $\square$

## 2. Lemma 22 (centred drift; the constant $K_\epsilon$)

$q_0^{-1}=b$, $q_m^{-1}=b+m+\sum_{j<m}\delta_j$. By Lemma 21, $q_m^{-1}-\tilde b_m=-\tfrac{29}{216}\sum_{j<m}(q_j-\tfrac1{b+j})-2\operatorname{Re}\bigl(a_{05}\sum_{j<m}q_je^{-6i\Theta_j}\bigr)+\sum_{j<m}R_j$. The three sums are bounded uniformly in $m$:

- $\sum_j|q_j-\tfrac1{b+j}|\le\sum_j\bar\epsilon_j/((b+j)(b+j-\bar\epsilon_j))\le\Lambda_{A_1}(0)$, part 1b's bracket with the sharper tails $\sum_{i\ge j}(b'+i)^{-2}\le(1+1/b')/(b'+j)$ and $\sum_{i\ge j}\log(1+i/2b')(b'+i)^{-2}\le[2\log(1+j/2b')+C_L]/(b'+j)$, $C_L=1+2(\log2+\tfrac1{8b'})$ (for $j\ge b'$: first term + integral, G1; for $j<b'$: the whole sum $\le\log2/b'+\sup$ summand, $\sup\le1/(8b'^2)$, and $b'+j\le2b'$). $\Lambda_{\rm iso}=2.5\times10^{-3}$ at $a=625$.
- Phase model: $\Theta_j=\Theta_0+\eta_\infty-\tau\log\tfrac{b+j}{b}+(\eta_j-\eta_\infty)$ with $|\eta_j-\eta_\infty|\le\tau\Lambda_{A_1}(j)+\tau/(b+j)+c_\theta\Sigma_2(j)$ (from $\Theta_{j+1}-\Theta_j=\operatorname{Arg}(1+u_j)$, $|\operatorname{Arg}(1+u_j)+\tau q_j|\le c_\theta q_j^2$, $\sum_{i<j}q_i=\sum_{i<j}(q_i-\tfrac1{b+i})+H_j(b)$ and $|\psi(x)-\log x|\le1/x$); the unknown constant $\eta_\infty$ factors out. Hence $|\sum_{j<m}q_je^{-6i\Theta_j}|\le|\zeta(s,b)-\zeta(s,b+m)|+\Lambda_{\rm iso}+6\sum_j\tfrac{|\eta_j-\eta_\infty|}{b+j}$, $s=1\pm6i\tau$, with $|\zeta(s,x)|\le\tfrac1{6\tau}+\tfrac{1+|s|}{2x}$ (first-order Euler–Maclaurin at $\operatorname{Re}s=1$; checked against mpmath's Hurwitz zeta at six points, both signs, G2) and the deviation sum majorised through $\sum_j(b'+j)^{-2}\le b'^{-2}+b'^{-1}$ and $\sum_j\log(1+j/2b')(b'+j)^{-2}\le\log2/b'+1/(8b'^2)$ (G1). $\Omega=0.360$ at $a=625$ (the $\zeta$ part $0.302$).
- $\sum_j|R_j|\le\gamma_2\sum_jq_j^2\le\gamma_2(2q_0+q_0^2)$ (one-orbit bound): $0.705$ at $a=625$.

$K_\epsilon=\tfrac{29}{216}\Lambda_{\rm iso}+2|a_{05}|\,\Omega+\gamma_2\Sigma_{2,0}$, evaluated at $b'=a-\Delta_a$ (the smallest admissible $b$; every sum decreases in $b$). $\square$ The constant is dominated by the $C_7$ ceiling through $\gamma_2$; with the B1-L2 ceiling $C_7\le85.1$ it would drop by about $15$ %; a Dyadic bound on $\operatorname{Re}(\rho_7/z)$ (or on $\sum_m\operatorname{Re}(\rho_{7,m}/z_m)$ along an orbit) is the lever.

## 3. Proposition 23 (centring identity) and Corollary 24 (log-free envelope)
