## 0. Summary

1. **Ledger.** RECOG and its sources name five "swap"/involution objects. They are **three mathematical objects and one undeclared physical candidate**: (i) $S_o=-I$ on the lift — exact, linear, global; the RRF wrapped inversion $\zeta\mapsto\zeta^{-1}$ is the same map in the wrapped profile (G8); (ii) $R_0=T^3F^{-1/2}$ — formal, local at $P_1$, the unique formal involution with linear part $-I$ commuting with $T$; $J=TF^{-1/6}$ is the formal order-6 cyclic generator ($J^6=\mathrm{id}$, $J^3=R_0$; v0.2.1 wording, after review comment #6389673617 — $J$ is not a "sixth root of $R_0$"); (iii) $S_h=h(-I)h^{-1}$ — an exact involution of the finite chart that equals $R_0$ through degree 6 and commutes with $F$ only through degree 6; (iv) the physical pole exchange $A\leftrightarrow B$ — no registered map; which of (i)–(iii), if any, represents it is RRF OPEN-A / OFN OPEN-OFN-OPA. Every RECOG statement is assigned to one of these (§1 table).
2. **Formal covariance is automatic (Prop. 1).** For *every* germ with $DT(0)=A$, $A^3=-I$, the map $R_0=T^3F^{-1/2}$ is a formal involution with linear part $-I$ commuting with $T$ (existence uses only the uniqueness of the tangent-to-identity formal square root; Dyadic's uniqueness of $R_0$ additionally needs $\Re\mu\,\Im\mu\ne0$). Certified for $T$, for the twins $T_s$, $T_0$, for $T_{-1}$ and for a perturbed germ with the same linear part (G3–G6). Hence the `[HYP]` "pole-swap covariance" has **no selective power** as a formal statement about an unspecified involution; it constrains $T$ only for a *declared* involution. R1's premise is therefore the question "which involution?", which is OPEN-A/OPA, not a RECOG derivation.
3. **$T_b$ measures $R_0\ne S_o$ (Prop. 2).** $R_0=-I$ iff $T$ is odd. For $T$: $R_0\ne-I$, and its degree-2 part is the unique solution of $r_2(Ax)-Ar_2(x)=2T_2(x)$, $T_2=(0,\rho^2/2)$ the quadratic (even) part of $T$; explicitly $r_2=(-\rho^2+\rho\eta-\tfrac12\eta^2,\ -\tfrac12\rho^2+\tfrac12\eta^2)$, and on the real $W$-ray $r_2=-\tfrac{3+i\sqrt3}{4}t^2=2c\,t^2$, reproducing $j^2S_h$ of RECOG-ALG-SH-SECTION-SHADOW and Dyadic's $[R_0]_2=2h_2$ (G3). ## 2. Statements

**Setting.** Lifted chart, $T(\rho,\eta)=(\rho-\sin\eta,\ \eta+e^\rho-1-\sin\eta)$, $A=DT(0)=\begin{pmatrix}1&-1\\1&0\end{pmatrix}$, $A^3=-I$, $F=T^6=\mathrm{id}+F_3+\cdots$ with no degree-2 term and $F_3=\mu|W|^2W$ in the $W$-frame (G1, exact in $\mathbb Q(\sqrt3)$). All identities below are formal, checked exactly to degree $N=8$ in $\mathbb Q[\rho,\eta]$.

**Proposition 1 (formal covariance is automatic).** `[ALG]` [scope: formal germs at $P_1$]. Let $T$ be any formal germ with $DT(0)=A$, $A^3=-I$ (so $F=T^6$ is tangent to the identity). Let $F^{-1/2}$ be the unique tangent-to-identity formal square root of $F^{-1}$ and $R_0:=T^3\circ F^{-1/2}$. Then $R_0^2=\mathrm{id}$, $R_0T=TR_0$, $DR_0(0)=-I$.
*Proof.* $T$ commutes with $F$, hence with the unique tangent-to-identity roots of $F^{\pm1}$ ($T\,F^{-1/2}\,T^{-1}$ is a tangent-to-identity square root of $F^{-1}$, so equals $F^{-1/2}$). Then $R_0^2=T^6F^{-1}=\mathrm{id}$, $R_0T=TR_0$, $DR_0=A^3=-I$. $\square$ Uniqueness of $R_0$ among such involutions is DYAD-THM-FORMAL-INVOLUTION-UNIQUE and needs $\Re\mu\ne0\ne\Im\mu$; it is not re-proved here. *Certified:* G2–G3 ($T$), G4 ($T_s$, $T_0$), G5 ($T_{-1}$), G6 (perturbed germ $\tilde T=T+$ rational quadratic and cubic terms, same $A$: involution, commutation, $DR_0=-I$ all hold). Controls: $A'^3=+I$ gives a tangent-to-identity involution, i.e. the identity — the $-I$ gate fails (NC1); the wrong root factor breaks $R_0^2=\mathrm{id}$ (NC2).
*Consequence.* "Pole-swap covariance" (RECOG §6.4 `[HYP]`) is vacuous for an unspecified involution: every such germ is formally covariant under its own $R_0$. It is selective only once an involution is declared — RECOG-ALG-SWAP-ODD-RHO declares $S_o$ on the radial row at linear order (where $S_o$ and $R_0$ agree) — and the declaration is OPEN-A/OPA's. R1's `[HYP]` sub-item sharpens to: *which involution represents the pole exchange?*

**Proposition 2 ($R_0=S_o$ iff $T$ is odd; $T_b$ measures the difference).** `[ALG]` [scope: formal]. $R_0=-I$ iff $T(-x)=-T(x)$. For the primitive $T$, $R_0\ne-I$: its degree-2 part is the unique solution of $r_2(Ax)-Ar_2(x)=2T_2(x)$ with $T_2=(0,\tfrac12\rho^2)$ (no degree-2 resonance at $p=6$), namely $r_2=(-\rho^2+\rho\eta-\tfrac12\eta^2,\ -\tfrac12\rho^2+\tfrac12\eta^2)$; on the real $W$-ray $r_2(t,0)=(-t^2,-\tfrac12t^2)$, i.e. $W'=-t-\tfrac{3+i\sqrt3}{4}t^2$, which is $j^2S_h$ (RECOG-ALG-SH-SECTION-SHADOW: $S_h(t)=-t+2ct^2$, $c=-\tfrac{3+i\sqrt3}8$) and Dyadic's $[R_0]_2=2h_2$. For $T_s$ and $T_0$ (odd), $R_0=S_o$ exactly.
*Proof.* If $T$ is odd, $-T^3$ is a tangent-to-identity square root of $F$, so $F^{1/2}=-T^3$ and $R_0=T^3(-T^3)^{-1}=-I$. Conversely $R_0=-I$ commuting with $T$ is oddness. Degree 2 of $R_0T=TR_0$: $-T_2(x)+r_2(Ax)=Ar_2(x)+T_2(-x)$ and $T_2$ is even. $\square$ *Certified:* G3, G4; G7 ($TS_o-S_oT=(0,2(\cosh\rho-1))$, zero for $T_s$).
*Consequence (wording).* In §6.6 ("breaks swap symmetry first at quadratic order"), §6.7 ($\mu_{\rm break}$, "symmetry-breaking-mediated") and §5 ("healing from symmetry breaking"), *symmetry* means $S_o$-symmetry. $T$ is formally symmetric under $R_0$. The radial fate is decided by $\mu$ (§6.7 radial-fate law) — the retired RECOG-DEF-BREAK-CAUSES-HEALING stays retired, now for a second reason.

**Proposition 3 (tangent-to-identity orientation equivalence).** `[ALG]` [scope: formal; character family $T_k$ of RECOG-ALG-SHEAR-ORIENTATION-CONJUGACY]. Let $R_0^{(k)}$ be the $R_0$ of $T_k$ and $\Phi_k:=S_o\circ R_0^{(k)}$. Then $D\Phi_k(0)=I$ and $\Phi_kT_k\Phi_k^{-1}=T_{-k}$; moreover $R_0^{(-k)}=S_oR_0^{(k)}S_o$.
*Proof.* $\Phi_kT_k\Phi_k^{-1}=S_oR_0T_kR_0^{-1}S_o=S_oT_kS_o=T_{-k}$ (RECOG-ALG-SHEAR-ORIENTATION-CONJUGACY) and $D\Phi_k=(-I)(-I)=I$. $\square$ *Certified:* G5 ($k=1$).
*Consequence.* The germs of $T_{+1}$ and $T_{-1}$ at $P_1$ are formally conjugate by a map that fixes the pole labels at linear order: their formal normal forms coincide (also directly: the resonant monomials of the $p=6$ normal form have odd degree). So no local formal invariant — multipliers, return-defect invariants, normal-form coefficients of any order — distinguishes the orientation, and OFN's reading of RECOG-ALG-SHEAR-ORIENTATION-CONJUGACY as "family conjugacy by a relabeling" is now complemented by a conjugacy that is not a relabeling. If $R_0$ converges (Dyadic OPEN 20, B-an), $\Phi$ is analytic and the statement is analytic. Orientation can therefore only be global, analytic or non-local structure.

**Proposition 3′ (local $C^\infty$ orientation equivalence; owner input).** `[ALG]` [assuming: DYAD-THM-SMOOTH-EMBEDDING (E4): a $C^\infty$ local involution $R$ at $P_1$ with $DR(0)=-I$, $RT=TR$ on the §6.15 domain $V$] [scope: $C^\infty$ local germs at $P_1$; the selected $T=T_1$ and its exact $S_o$-conjugate $T_{-1}$]. Let $\Phi_\infty:=S_o\circ R$. Then $\Phi_\infty$ is a $C^\infty$ local diffeomorphism with $\Phi_\infty(0)=0$, $D\Phi_\infty(0)=(-I)(-I)=I$, and on $V$: $\Phi_\infty T_1\Phi_\infty^{-1}=S_oRT_1R^{-1}S_o=S_oT_1S_o=T_{-1}$.
*Proof.* $S_o$ is a global linear involution, so $\Phi_\infty^{-1}=R^{-1}S_o=RS_o$; the rest is $RT_1=T_1R$ (E4) and RECOG-ALG-SHEAR-ORIENTATION-CONJUGACY. $\square$ Since $j^\infty R=R_0$ (Dyadic §6.16–6.17), $j^\infty\Phi_\infty=\Phi$ of Prop. 3; no new computation is needed. *Not supplied:* analyticity of $R$ or $\Phi_\infty$ (B-an, OPEN 20); a numerical radius for $V$ (Dyadic records none); any statement about a physical orientation carrier beyond the bare family $T_{\pm1}$.
*Consequence.* Local smooth conjugacy invariants of the bare family cannot carry the orientation sign. Combined with Prop. 3 this is the strongest form RECOG can register from owner inputs; it does not exclude orientation being carried by a global, analytic or non-dyad structure.

**Proposition 4 (the wrapped inversion is $S_o$).** `[ALG]`. $\zeta^{-1}=e^{-\rho-i\eta}$, so the inversion of the RRF wrapped native ratio is $S_o$ on the lift; under $S_o$ applied to both states $C_\to(a,b)=\overline W_aW_b$ is invariant, $\widehat C_{\rm pole}\mapsto\widehat C_{\rm pole}^{-1}$, $\rho\mapsto-\rho$, $Q\mapsto Q$ (G8). Hence the "five objects" of the study plan are three maps ($S_o$, $R_0$, $S_h$), one formal root ($J$), and one undeclared physical candidate.
