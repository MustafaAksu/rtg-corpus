## 0. Summary

1. **Algorithm (Prop. 20, a certified computation).** Record-only inputs: $n_0$, the slips $(k,M_k,r_k)$, $x_0\in U$. Start region: the Lemma-8 interval $[a^-,a^+]$ (first two slips, $|h-D\tfrac\pi3|\le0.62$). For an $a$-box $A=[a_1,a_2]$ the **contractor** runs: $\omega=a-b\in[-\Delta_A,\Delta_A]$, $\Delta_A=\tfrac{16}{15}c_Q\sqrt{a_2}$; $L$-window $I=[\max_k\inf_A(c'''_k-E'''_k),\min_k\sup_A(c'''_k+E'''_k)]$; then three rounds of: $\Theta_0$-arc $=$ Lemma-16 sector$(n_0,A)$ $\cap$ Prop.-18 arc$(A,I,b)$ (on the circle; an arc of width $\ge2\pi$ is no constraint) $\to$ $\omega$-interval from Lemma 17 with $\sqrt A$ and $\beta(A)$ (intersected with the previous one) $\to$ $b=A-\omega$ $\to$ centred constraints $c^{(4)}_k=c'''_k(A)-\tau\,{\rm mid}(\omega S_{M_k})$, $E^{(4)}_k=E'''_k(A)-\tau A_0(A,M_k)+\tau\,{\rm half}(\omega S_{M_k})$ $\to$ new $I$ (intersected). A box is **excluded** when any window or arc becomes empty; otherwise it is bisected down to $w_{\min}=\tfrac14$ and kept. The union $\mathcal U$ of kept boxes is an outer enclosure of the admissible $a$-set; the hulls of the kept boxes' final $I$ and $\omega$, and the circular hull (§1a) of their $\Theta_0$-arcs, enclose the admissible $L$, start offset and start angle.
2. **Invariant.** The true $(a,L,b,\Theta_0,\omega)$ satisfies every enclosure at every step of every box containing $a$ (all maps are enclosure-valid on the box: Lemma 16, Prop. 18, Lemma 17, the $S_M$ bracket, the interval evaluation of $c'''$, $E'''$, $A_0$, and the outer window), hence the true $a$ is never excluded and the true $L$, $\omega$, $\Theta_0$ lie in the hulls. No inner (sure) test is attempted for the joint region, so no statement about the exact admissible set, uniqueness, or injectivity is made.
3. **Results (`[NUM]`, 12 records).** Record-only outer $a$-enclosures of width $5$–$21$ (combined-tail cell: $6$–$114$; part 2: $173$–$965$), $L$-hulls $0.7$–$2.8\times10^{-4}$ rad, $\omega=a-b$ hulls of width $1.6$–$2.2$ (uncentred $2\Delta_a=42$–$85$), $\Theta_0$-hulls $0.004$–$0.023$ rad; true $a$, reference $L$ and true $\theta_{W,0}$ inside in all records (G1); every enclosure a single interval (§2).
4. **Typing.** Same dependencies as the start-offset cell ((D5) $c_Q,c'_Q$; R9; the Dyadic rows of the previous cells); the enclosure is an outer approximation (undecided boxes of width $\le\tfrac14$ at the ends and possibly inside; no distance bound to the exact set, as in part 2 v0.2.1).

## 1. Proposition 20 (contractor; validity)

For a box $A$ and a record, define the maps of §0 item 1. Each is an enclosure-valid map on $A$: (i) $c'''_k(A)$, $E'''_k(A)$, $A_0(A,M_k)$ are interval evaluations of the combined-tail and part-1b formulas, which hold pointwise for every $a\in A$; (ii) the Lemma-16 sector with $\bar\alpha_0$ evaluated on $A$ contains $\Theta_0$ for every $a\in A$; (iii) the Prop.-18 arc with $L\in I$, $b\in[b^-,b^+]$ and the R9 memory interval contains $\Theta_0$ whenever $(L,b)$ are admissible; (iv) the Lemma-17 interval with $\sqrt A$ and $\beta(A)$ contains $\omega$ for every $a\in A$ and $\Theta_0$ in the arc; (v) $S_M(a,b)\in[\tfrac1{c^++M},\tfrac1{(c^-+M)^2}+\tfrac1{c^-+M}]$ with $c^+=\max(a_2,b^+)$, $c^-=\min(a_1,b^-)$; (vi) the centred window is the outer window of $|c^{(4)}_k(a)-L|\le E^{(4)}_k(a)$ over $a\in A$, valid by the combined-tail identity ($\varepsilon'''_k$ contains $+\tau\omega S_{M_k}$ exactly up to the $A_1$-bounded remainder). Intersections with previous enclosures keep validity. Hence if the true $a\in A$, no step empties, and the box is kept with the true $L,\omega,\Theta_0$ in its final enclosures. $\square$

### 1a. Arcs on the circle (JT-1, JT-2; v0.2)

An arc is a real pair $(\ell,h)$ with $h-\ell<2\pi$ read modulo $2\pi$; `FULL` denotes the whole circle. Every arc entering the contractor passes through `arc_or_full`: an arc of width $\ge2\pi$ (whether the Lemma-16 sector on a wide or low box or the Prop.-18 arc) is replaced by `FULL`, and `arc_meet` treats `FULL` as the identity, the empty set as absorbing, and otherwise intersects over all $2\pi$-branches (`circ_intersect`, SO-2). The $\Theta_0$-enclosure of a box starts at `FULL`; when it stays `FULL`, $\Phi$ (a $2\pi$-periodic trigonometric polynomial) is ranged over $[-\pi,\pi]$, so the Lemma-17 $\omega$-interval is then the uninformative $\pm\sup|\Phi|\sqrt a\pm\beta$. No assertion on arc widths remains on the contractor path, so a wide sector produces a conservative (weaker) enclosure, never an exception (JT-1). Independently of this branch, the sector width $\tfrac\pi3+(2n_0+1)\alpha_0+2D_0$ is increasing in $\sup Q=1/\inf A$ (with $\bar\alpha_0$ increasing, checked on a grid of $2000$ points of $[0,Q_{U_T}]$) and the capped iterate makes $A=[625,10^{12}]$ the worst case of every admissible box: its widths are $1.16,1.22,1.29,1.36,1.44,1.51,1.58$ rad for $n_0=1,\dots,7$, all $<2\pi$ (`[NUM]`, JT1 in the JSON) — so in the certificate run the branch is never taken, and the control of §5 exercises it synthetically.

The reported $\Theta_0$-hull is the **circular hull** of the kept boxes' arcs (`circ_hull`): each arc is normalised to start in $[0,2\pi)$, the union is formed on the circle, and the hull is the complement of the largest gap of that union (`FULL` if the union covers the circle); it is reported as a representative with left end in $[-\pi,\pi)$. The construction does not depend on the $2\pi$-representatives the boxes happen to carry, so the hull is a certified enclosing arc of the admissible start angles (JT-2). In the certificate run every kept arc is a sub-arc of the record's sector representative (`circ_intersect` returns pieces clipped to its first argument), so the circular hull coincides with the naive real hull of v0.1; the JSON stores both widths (`Theta0_width`, `Theta0_width_naive`) and the G1 angle test uses the circular hull widened by $D_0^+$.

## 3. Consequences and next steps

- The unknown-$a$ decoder now returns, from the record alone, a certified $a$-window at the scale of the start-offset residual plus the in-block residual at the first slip, and an $L$-window close to the known-$a$ one; nothing about uniqueness.
- Remaining levers (unchanged): the in-block $c_P$ bookkeeping at $r\ge3$ slips; centring the logarithmic chart drift; part 3's Q1′ remains open.

## 4. Not claimed

No exact admissible set; no inner enclosure; no uniqueness or injectivity; no universal performance theorem (widths are per-record computations); the reference $L$ used in G1 is a simulation estimate with its stated tolerance.

## 5. Certificate

G1 containment (true $a$ in $\mathcal U$; reference $L$ in the $L$-hull within tolerance; true $\theta_{W,0}$ in the $\Theta_0$-hull within $D_0^+$) on 12/12 records.
