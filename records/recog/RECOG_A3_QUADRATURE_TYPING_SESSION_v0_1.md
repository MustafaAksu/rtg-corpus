## 0. Summary

1. **Typing in the primitive chart (exact).** On the quadrature $\mathcal Q=\{\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole}=0\}=\{\cos\eta=0\}$ the one-step Jacobian is the nontrivial unipotent shear $J=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\e^\rho&1\end{pmatrix}$ with $1$-eigenspace the comparator direction $\partial_\eta$ (Q2). The discriminant identity $\Delta_J=c(c-4e^\rho)$, $c=\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole}$, is re-verified (Q1).
2. **What is chart-free about $\mathcal Q$ (comparator geometry).** Three characterizations in terms of the dyad observables $(\rho,\eta)$ only — none of them mentions eigenvalues:
   (a) $\rho(Tx)-\rho(x)=-\operatorname{Im}\widehat C_{\rm pole}(x)$, so $|\rho(Tx)-\rho(x)|\le1$ with equality **exactly on $\mathcal Q$**: the quadrature is the locus of extremal one-step radial transfer;
   (b) $\partial_\eta[\rho\circ T-\rho]=-\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole}$: $\mathcal Q$ is the critical locus of the radial transfer along the comparator fiber (the radial row decouples from $\eta$ to first order);
   (c) with the *declared* area form $\Omega=d\rho\wedge d\eta=\dfrac{d\bar\zeta\wedge d\zeta}{2i|\zeta|^2}$ on the RRF wrapped native ratio $\zeta=e^{\rho+i\eta}\in\mathbb C^*$ (the multiplicative Haar form; swap-invariant): $T^*\Omega=(1+(e^\rho-1)\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole})\,\Omega$, so $\mathcal Q\cup\{\rho=0\}$ is the $\Omega$-area-neutral locus of the one-step transport (Q3). Scope: `[ALG] [scope: derived / model-intrinsic on the RRF wrapped profile relative to the declared Ω; Ω is transported with any chart change; not an RRF-registered physical observable; no OPEN-A bearing]`.
   So the "coordinate-free counterpart via comparator geometry" asked for in R4 sub-item 1 exists, but it is a *transfer/area* statement about the named native observables $(\rho,\eta)$ (a chart change is not permission to re-read the new coordinate as $\rho$), not a *type* statement.
3. **Why the typing alone is not intrinsic (explicit).** Two global unimodular charts retype $\mathcal Q$ (Q4): chart A, $\varphi_A=(\rho+\varepsilon\sin\eta,\ \eta)$, gives on $\mathcal Q$ a Jacobian with $\det=1$ and $\operatorname{tr}=2+\varepsilon e^\rho\cos\eta'$, hence **hyperbolic** for $\varepsilon=+\tfrac12$ ($\operatorname{tr}=2.4207$ at $\rho=0$) and **elliptic** for $\varepsilon=-\tfrac12$ ($\operatorname{tr}=1.5793$); chart B, $\varphi_B=(\rho,\ \eta+\tfrac e{e-1}e^\rho)$, makes the Jacobian the **identity** on the whole branch $\eta=\pi/2$ (the shear strength $e^\rho$ is a chart artefact). A genuine conjugacy (constant linear chart) preserves $\operatorname{tr},\det$ everywhere (NC1). This is SG-R3 made explicit on $\mathcal Q$: pointwise, off fixed points, only the rank and the sign of $\det$ survive a chart change; "parabolic" does not.
4. **$(t,d)$-plane route.** Multipliers are intrinsic only on periodic points. A quadrature point on an orbit contributes the factor $\det J=1$ to $d_n$ and no separable factor to $t_n$ (two-step example, Q5). So **no signature of $\mathcal Q$ is forced by the displayed local identities alone**; whether some periodic orbit passes through $\mathcal Q$ is not decided here. The `[NUM]` probe (periods $\le8$, $|L|\le2$, $\rho\in[-2.5,2.5]$, finite seed grid) finds 18 orbits, none with a point on $\mathcal Q$ (closest $|\cos\eta|=0.031$) — a sampled finite search, not a nonexistence result.
5. **By-product for R4 sub-item 2 (exact, wrapped cylinder only).** Inside the pole-comparator inversion-fixed value stratum $\Sigma=\{\widehat C_{\rm pole}=\pm1\}=\{\sin\eta=0\}$ (the set where the comparator value is fixed under $C\mapsto C^{-1}$; invariant under the state swap $S$ as a set, **not** pointwise fixed by $S$), $\rho$ is preserved and $\eta\mapsto\eta+e^\rho-1$; hence the wrapped-periodic orbits contained in $\Sigma$ are exactly $e^\rho-1=m\pi$, $m\ge0$: $m=0$ gives $P_1,P_2$; $m=2k$ the known winding fixed points; **$m$ odd gives new period-2 orbits of the wrapped map** $\{(\ln(1+m\pi),0),(\ln(1+m\pi),\pi)\}$ alternating between the pole-level values $\widehat C_{\rm pole}=+1$ and $-1$, with $(\operatorname{tr}M_2,\det M_2)=(1,\,1-m^2\pi^2)$ — hyperbolic and orientation-reversing (Q6; the probe finds $m=1$ to $10^{-10}$). On the unwrapped lift these are not periodic: $\eta$ advances by $2m\pi$ over the two steps. A scoped addition to the *partial* inventory; no completeness outside $\Sigma$.

## 1. Objects and conventions

Primitive map in the lifted chart (Dyadic §6.1): $T(\rho,\eta)=(\rho-\sin\eta,\ \eta+e^\rho-1-\sin\eta)$; $(\rho,\eta)$ are the dyad observables (log amplitude ratio, relative phase), not an arbitrary chart. Pole comparator (RECOG-DEF-POLE-COMPARATOR): $\widehat C_{\rm pole}=e^{i\eta}$; pole swap $S(\rho,\eta)=(-\rho,-\eta)$ sends $\widehat C_{\rm pole}\mapsto\overline{\widehat C_{\rm pole}}=\widehat C_{\rm pole}^{-1}$. RRF wrapped native ratio (rrf-v0.8.4 §2.1): $\zeta=e^{\rho+i\eta}\in\mathbb C^*$; lifted $(\rho,\eta)$ (§2.2). Declared area form: $\Omega=d\rho\wedge d\eta=d(\log|\zeta|)\wedge d(\arg\zeta)=\dfrac{d\bar\zeta\wedge d\zeta}{2i|\zeta|^2}$, the multiplicative Haar form on $\mathbb C^*$; $S^*\Omega=\Omega$. Ω is a *declared* reference form of this record, transported with any chart change; it is not registered by RRF as a physical observable. Quadrature: $\mathcal Q=\{\widehat C_{\rm pole}=\pm i\}=\{\cos\eta=0\}$, two branches $\eta=\pm\pi/2$ mod $2\pi$, swapped by $S$ (so $\mathcal Q$ is swap-invariant as a set). Jacobian $J=DT=\begin{pmatrix}1&-c\\e^\rho&1-c\end{pmatrix}$, $c=\cos\eta$. A *chart* here is a diffeomorphism $\varphi$ of the lifted plane respecting $\eta$-periodicity; the transformed one-step Jacobian at $x$ is $D\varphi_{Tx}\,J_x\,D\varphi_x^{-1}$ (SG-R3). "Type" means elliptic/parabolic/hyperbolic of a $2\times2$ real matrix.

## 2. Statements

**Proposition 1 (primitive-chart typing of $\mathcal Q$).** `[ALG]` target [scope: primitive chart]. $\operatorname{tr}J=2-c$, $\det J=1+(e^\rho-1)c$, $\Delta_J=c(c-4e^\rho)$ (as RECOG §6.5). On $\mathcal Q$: $J=\begin{pmatrix}1&0\\e^\rho&1\end{pmatrix}$, $(J-I)\ne0$, $(J-I)^2=0$, $J\partial_\eta=\partial_\eta$. Both branches identical. *Certified Q1–Q2.* $\square$

**Proposition 2 (comparator-geometric characterizations).** `[ALG]` target [scope: dyad observables $(\rho,\eta)$; no chart]. With $\Delta\rho:=\rho\circ T-\rho$:
(a) $\Delta\rho=-\operatorname{Im}\widehat C_{\rm pole}$; $|\Delta\rho|\le1$ with equality iff $x\in\mathcal Q$ ($\Delta\rho=-1$ on $\eta=\pi/2$, $+1$ on $\eta=-\pi/2$).
(b) $\partial_\eta\Delta\rho=-\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole}$, zero iff $x\in\mathcal Q$.
(c) `[ALG] [scope: derived / model-intrinsic on the RRF wrapped profile relative to the declared Ω]` $T^*\Omega=\bigl(1+(e^\rho-1)\operatorname{Re}\widehat C_{\rm pole}\bigr)\Omega$; $S^*\Omega=\Omega$ ($\det DS=+1$); the one-step transport is Ω-expanding where $(e^\rho-1)\cos\eta>0$, Ω-contracting where $<0$, Ω-neutral exactly on $\mathcal Q\cup\{\rho=0\}$.
*Certified Q3.* Remarks: (a)–(b) are statements about the named native observables $\rho$ and $\eta$ and survive any relabelling that fixes those observables; a chart change does not license re-reading the new coordinate as $\rho$. (c) is geometric only relative to Ω: the multiplier $T^*\Omega/\Omega$ is chart-independent when Ω is transported with the chart, whereas the plain determinant of an arbitrarily chosen coordinate Jacobian is not (if the reference form is silently redefined the value changes). None of (a)–(c) is a statement about eigenvalues of $DT$. Whether Ω is an observable is an RRF question (informational interface question to route; no OPEN-A bearing; no physical/intrinsic metric claimed). $\square$

**Proposition 3 (the parabolic type is not chart-invariant on $\mathcal Q$).** `[ALG]` target.
(i) *General form (standard fact, cited, not re-proved here):* for $x$ with $Tx\ne x$ — true on all of $\mathcal Q$ since $|\Delta\rho|=1$ — and any $A\in GL^+(2,\mathbb R)$ there is a chart $\varphi$ with $D(\varphi T\varphi^{-1})_{\varphi(x)}=A$, because $\operatorname{Diff}$ acts transitively on oriented frames at two distinct points; hence the only pointwise chart-invariants are the rank and $\operatorname{sign}\det$ (SG-R3).
(ii) *Explicit witnesses (certified Q4):* chart A, $\varphi_A(\rho,\eta)=(\rho+\varepsilon\sin\eta,\eta)$, $\det D\varphi_A\equiv1$: transformed $\operatorname{tr}=2-c+\varepsilon e^\rho(\cos\eta'-\cos\eta)$ with $\eta'=\eta+e^\rho-1-\sin\eta$, $\det$ unchanged; on $\mathcal Q$: $\operatorname{tr}=2+\varepsilon e^\rho\cos\eta'$, $\det=1$. At $(\rho,\eta)=(0,\pi/2)$: $\cos\eta'=\sin1>0$, so $\varepsilon=\tfrac12$ gives $\operatorname{tr}=2.4207$ (hyperbolic) and $\varepsilon=-\tfrac12$ gives $\operatorname{tr}=1.5793$ (elliptic). Chart B, $\varphi_B(\rho,\eta)=(\rho,\eta+\alpha e^\rho)$, $\alpha=\tfrac e{e-1}$: on the branch $\eta=\pi/2$ (where $\rho'=\rho-1$) the transformed Jacobian is $I$; on $\eta=-\pi/2$ it is the shear of strength $e^\rho(1+e)$. Both charts are global unimodular shears of the lifted plane and descend to the cylinder.
(iii) *Negative controls:* a constant linear chart preserves $\operatorname{tr}$ and $\det$ identically (NC1); chart A at $\varepsilon=0$ reproduces Proposition 1 (NC2).
*Consequence:* "$\mathcal Q$ is parabolic" is a primitive-chart sentence; the chart-free content of $\mathcal Q$ is Proposition 2. $\square$

**Proposition 4 (no signature of $\mathcal Q$ forced by the local identities).** `[ALG]` target for the identity; `[NUM]` for the probe. For a period-$n$ point, $(t_n,d_n)=(\operatorname{tr}M_n,\det M_n)$ is chart-invariant and $d_n=\prod_i\det J_{x_i}$; a point of the orbit on $\mathcal Q$ contributes the factor $1$ to $d_n$ and no separable factor to $t_n$ (two-step example: $\operatorname{tr}(J_{Tx}J_x)|_{\eta=\pi/2}=2+(1+e^\rho)\sin(e^\rho-2)$ depends on the partner point; Q5). Hence no universal multiplier signature of $\mathcal Q$ can be deduced from quadrature membership alone. This is not a nonexistence statement: whether periodic orbits through $\mathcal Q$ exist (globally or in the probe's box) is undecided; the sampled finite search of §3 found none up to period 8. $\square$

**Proposition 5 (wrapped-periodic orbits inside the pole-comparator inversion-fixed value stratum).** `[ALG]` target [scope: wrapped cylinder]. $\Sigma:=\{\widehat C_{\rm pole}=\pm1\}=\{\sin\eta=0\}$ is the set of states whose pole-comparator *value* is fixed under the inversion $C\mapsto C^{-1}$ (RECOG §6.5 calls it the pole-level pair-symmetry stratum); it is $S$-invariant as a set but not pointwise fixed by $S$ ($S$ fixes only $\rho=0$ there). On $\Sigma$: $T(\rho,k\pi)=(\rho,k\pi+e^\rho-1)$. A wrapped-periodic orbit contained in $\Sigma$ therefore has $e^\rho-1\in\pi\mathbb Z_{\ge0}$, and conversely every $\rho_m=\ln(1+m\pi)$, $m\ge0$, gives one: $m=0$: $P_1=(0,0)$, $P_2=(0,\pi)$; $m=2k$: the winding fixed points $(\rho_{2k},0)$ with $(t,d)=(1,1+2\pi k)$ and $(\rho_{2k},\pi)$ with $(t,d)=(3,1-2\pi k)$; $m$ odd: the period-2 orbits $(\rho_m,0)\leftrightarrow(\rho_m,\pi)$ with $M_2=J_{(\rho_m,\pi)}J_{(\rho_m,0)}$, $(t_2,d_2)=(1,\,1-m^2\pi^2)$, hyperbolic ($\Delta=4m^2\pi^2-3>0$) and orientation-reversing. These are periodic for the wrapped map only: on the lift $\eta$ advances by $2m\pi$ over the two steps. No point of $\Sigma$ lies on $\mathcal Q$. *Certified Q6 (symbolic $m$, $k$).* Remark: the odd-$m$ orbits alternate between the pole-level values $\widehat C_{\rm pole}=+1$ and $-1$; they are a scoped addition to the *partial* periodic inventory of the primitive $T$ beyond the fixed points recorded in RECOG/Dyadic — complete inside $\Sigma$, with no completeness claim outside it. Alternating pole values are not recognition events (SG-R6). $\square$
