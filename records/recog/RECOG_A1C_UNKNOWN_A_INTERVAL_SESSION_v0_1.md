## 0. Summary

1. **Setting.** $x_0\in U$ (so $a=Q_0^{-1}\ge625$) with $a$ unknown; the record supplies the slips $(k,M_k)_{k\le K}$ ($M_k\ge1$). The THM gives $c_k(a):=\tau\psi(a+M_k)-(k-1)\tfrac\pi3=L+\varepsilon_k$ with $|\varepsilon_k|\le E'(a,M_k)$ when $a$ is the true value. Hence (Prop. 4) the true pair lies in the **admissible region** $\mathcal R_K=\{(a,L):a\ge625,\ |c_k(a)-L|\le E'(a,M_k)\ \forall k\}$ and the true $a$ in its projection $\mathcal A_K=\{a\ge625:\max_j(c_j-E'_j)(a)\le\min_j(c_j+E'_j)(a)\}$ (all pairwise conditions; 1-D Helly). Nothing here needs monotonicity of $E'$ in $a$.
2. **First finite interval (Lemma 8, the reviewer's simplification).** For two slips $k_1<k_2$, $h(a)=\tau[\psi(a+M_{k_2})-\psi(a+M_{k_1})]$ is strictly decreasing and $|h(a)-(k_2-k_1)\tfrac\pi3|\le E'_1+E'_2\le0.62$; since $(k_2-k_1)\tfrac\pi3-0.62\ge0.427>0$, $\mathcal A_K\subset[a^-,a^+]=h^{-1}([D\tfrac\pi3-0.62,D\tfrac\pi3+0.62])\cap[625,\infty)$ — finite, computed by bisection with a rigorous digamma bracket (Lemma 7). On the 12 sampled orbits (first two slips): $[625,2418]$ … $[625,14981]$.
3. **Inner and outer enclosures of $\mathcal A_K$ and the $L$-projection (Prop. 5).** Interval boxes over $[a^-,a^+]$: a box is *excluded* when some pair has $\inf(c_j-E'_j)>\sup(c_k+E'_k)$ on it, *sure* when $\max_j\sup(c_j-E'_j)\le\min_k\inf(c_k+E'_k)$, otherwise bisected down to width $w_{\min}=\tfrac14$. Outer enclosure $\mathcal U\supset\mathcal A_K$ = sure boxes $\cup$ small undecided boxes; inner enclosure $\mathcal U_{\rm in}\subset\mathcal A_K$ = sure boxes; all float endpoints rounded outward (outer) resp. inward (inner), after merging adjacent boxes in exact arithmetic (boxes are merged only when they overlap or share an exact dyadic endpoint; a positive gap is never bridged). $w_{\min}$ is the stopping resolution of the bisection, not a bound on the distance between $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal A_K$: interval over-estimation can leave undecided boxes anywhere, so the only certified statements are $\mathcal U_{\rm in}\subset\mathcal A_K\subset\mathcal U$, with the measured total undecided length as the gap ($0.4$–$1.5$ on the sampled records). The $L$-hull of $\mathcal U$ contains the $L$-projection of $\mathcal R_K$. Results (`[NUM]` orbits, all slips of a $1.8\times10^6$-step run): outer and inner enclosures a single interval each in every case, differing by $\le1.5$; $a$-interval widths $173$–$245$ for $a=625$ ($28$–$39$ % of $a$), $461$–$589$ for $a=1000$ ($46$–$59$ %), $726$–$965$ for $a=2500$ ($29$–$39$ %); the true $a$ inside in 12/12; the $L$-hull has width $0.022$–$0.035$ and contains the estimated $L$ in 12/12 — essentially the known-$a$ last window ($2E'(a,M_K)\approx0.025$), because $c_K(a)$ varies by only $\approx\tau\,\Delta a/(a+M_K)\lesssim10^{-3}$ across the $a$-interval.
4. **What limits the width.** The widths shrink with $K$ ($537\to173$ for the first $a=625$ orbit) but saturate: the binding pair is always (first slip, latest slip), and the width tends to $\approx2E'(a,M_1)\,(a+M_1)/\tau$ — set by the envelope at the **earliest** slip, where $E'\approx0.25$ and the in-block term is $65$ % of it. Relative width $\approx c/\sqrt a$ improves with $a$. This is a certified parameter window; integer recovery of $a$ is not in reach by this route (and is not claimed), consistent with part 1b's feasibility estimate ($\pm200$–$260$ for $a=625$).
5. **Dependencies.** Everything is conditional exactly as part 1b: `[assuming: Dyadic row-A bridge/remainder constants as published]` through $E'$; RECOG-side otherwise (THM, digamma bracket, elementary interval analysis). Grade target `[ALG]` for Props. 4–5 and Lemma 8; the algorithm is a certified computation (outer enclosure), its output per record is `[RESULT]`-type data.
6. **Scope.** No injectivity (part 3); no claim about the decoder's behaviour outside $U$ or for records with fewer than two slips with $M_k\ge1$; no event/WRITE statement (SG-R6).

## 1. Statements and proofs

**Lemma 7 (digamma bracket).** For $x>0$: $\ln x-\tfrac1{2x}-\tfrac1{12x^2}\le\psi(x)\le\ln x-\tfrac1{2x}-\tfrac1{12x^2}+\tfrac1{120x^4}$. *Proof.* Stirling series of $\psi$ with the alternating-remainder property for real $x>0$ (the remainder after the $B_2$ term has the sign and at most the size of the $B_4$ term). $\square$ G1: the interval bracket contains $\psi$ (70-digit reference) at 2000 points of $[625,5\times10^6]$; width $\le5.5\times10^{-14}$. Used as $\psi(X)\subset[\inf f(X),\sup(f+\tfrac1{120x^4})(X)]$ for interval $X$ (outward rounding).

**Proposition 4 (admissible region).** `[ALG]` target [scope: $x_0\in U$; slip record $(k,M_k)_{k\le K}$ with $M_k\ge1$; $E'$ of part 1b] For the true $a$ and $L$ of the orbit, $(a,L)\in\mathcal R_K$ and $a\in\mathcal A_K$; $\mathcal A_K$ is the $a$-projection of $\mathcal R_K$. *Proof.* THM (S) with part 1b Cor. 3: $|c_k(a)-L|\le E'(a,M_k)$ for every $k$. For fixed $a$ the $L$-constraints are intervals $[c_k-E'_k,c_k+E'_k]$; their intersection is non-empty iff $\max_j(c_j-E'_j)\le\min_j(c_j+E'_j)$. $\square$

**Lemma 8 (first finite interval).** Let $k_1<k_2$ be two slips, $D=k_2-k_1\ge1$, $h(a):=\tau[\psi(a+M_{k_2})-\psi(a+M_{k_1})]$. Then $h$ is strictly decreasing on $[625,\infty)$ ($h'(a)=\tau[\psi_1(a+M_{k_2})-\psi_1(a+M_{k_1})]<0$, trigamma decreasing), $h\to0$, and $\mathcal A_K\subset\{a\ge625:|h(a)-D\tfrac\pi3|\le0.62\}=[a^-,a^+]$ with $a^-=\max(625,h^{-1}(D\tfrac\pi3+0.62))$, $a^+=h^{-1}(D\tfrac\pi3-0.62)<\infty$. *Proof.* $a\in\mathcal A_K$ gives $c_{k_1}(a)-E'_1\le c_{k_2}(a)+E'_2$ and $c_{k_2}-E'_2\le c_{k_1}+E'_1$, i.e. $|c_{k_2}(a)-c_{k_1}(a)|\le E'_1+E'_2\le0.62$ (part 1b Lemma 6), and $c_{k_2}-c_{k_1}=h(a)-D\tfrac\pi3$. $D\tfrac\pi3-0.62\ge\tfrac\pi3-0.62=0.427>0$ makes the preimage bounded. $\square$ Computation: bisection on $a$ with the interval $h$, outer rounding ($a^-$ rounded down, $a^+$ up; tolerance $10^{-6}$). Note the reviewer's form $g=-h$: $g'(a)>0$.

**Proposition 5 (outer enclosure by boxes).** For a box $X\subset[a^-,a^+]$ let $\underline c_k,\overline c_k$ and $\underline E_k,\overline E_k$ be interval enclosures of $c_k$, $E'_k$ on $X$. If $\max_j(\underline c_j-\overline E_j)>\min_k(\overline c_k+\overline E_k)$ then $X\cap\mathcal A_K=\emptyset$ (**excluded**); if $\max_j(\overline c_j-\underline E_j)\le\min_k(\underline c_k+\underline E_k)$ then $X\subset\mathcal A_K$ (**sure**). Bisecting undecided boxes down to width $w_{\min}$ terminates; the union $\mathcal U$ of kept boxes satisfies $\mathcal A_K\subset\mathcal U$, and $\bigcup_{X\in\mathcal U}[\max_j\inf_X(c_j-E'_j),\ \min_k\sup_X(c_k+E'_k)]\supset\{L:(a,L)\in\mathcal R_K\}$. *Proof.* Inclusion monotonicity of interval arithmetic; for $a\in X\cap\mathcal A_K$, $\max_j(c_j-E'_j)(a)\ge\max_j\inf_X(\cdot)$ and $\min_k(c_k+E'_k)(a)\le\min_k\sup_X(\cdot)$, which is impossible under the exclusion test; the sure test is the condition at every point of $X$; the $L$-hull per box contains $\bigcap_k[c_k-E'_k,c_k+E'_k](a)$ for every $a\in X$. $\square$ The sure boxes give the inner enclosure $\mathcal U_{\rm in}\subset\mathcal A_K$. Neither enclosure is the exact set: $w_{\min}$ is only the stopping resolution of the bisection (an undecided box of width $\le w_{\min}$ may lie entirely outside $\mathcal A_K$, and interval over-estimation can leave undecided boxes in the interior), so no distance bound between $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal A_K$ is claimed; the certified content is $\mathcal U_{\rm in}\subset\mathcal A_K\subset\mathcal U$ with the reported undecided length as the gap. $\mathcal A_K$ is not claimed to be an interval, though $\mathcal U$ and $\mathcal U_{\rm in}$ were single intervals in every sampled case. Float endpoints: $\mathcal U$'s are rounded outward and $\mathcal U_{\rm in}$'s inward (`fdn`/`fup`, one ulp), as are the Lemma-8 ends.

**Width heuristic `[SESSION]`.** For the pair (first slip $k_1$, slip $k$): the constraint $|h(a)-D\tfrac\pi3|\le E'_1+E'_k$ with $|h'(a)|\approx\tau[(a+M_1)^{-1}-(a+M_k)^{-1}]$ gives a width $\approx2(E'_1+E'_k)/|h'|\to2E'(a,M_1)(a+M_1)/\tau$ as $M_k\to\infty$; with $E'(a,M)\approx5.5$–$7/\sqrt{a+M}$ this is $\approx(10$–$12)\sqrt{a+M_1}$ — e.g. $a=625$, $M_1=178$: $\approx300$ (observed $173$ with all seven slips, the joint constraints doing somewhat better than the single pair). Later slips reduce $E'_k$ but not $E'_1$; the earliest slip is the bottleneck, and its $E'$ is dominated by the in-block term ($5\arcsin(0.705\sqrt{Q^*})$).

## 3. Consequences and next steps

- **For the RESULT / OPEN R6 A1c-decoder:** the record determines, without knowledge of $a$, a certified finite interval for $a$ (Lemma 8, then Prop. 5) and a certified interval for $L$ of width $\approx2E'(a,M_K)$ — i.e. the phase readout loses almost nothing from not knowing $a$. Proposal for a later release: the RESULT's "a-priori envelope" clause becomes "computable from the record alone by Props. 4–5 (conditional on (D5))". Not adopted.
- **Narrowing the $a$-window** requires a better envelope at the *earliest* slip: the in-block term (part 1b §5) is the lever; a second lever is a two-sided (signed) use of the Lemma-C′ deficit at the anchor instead of its modulus. Both are RECOG-side cells.
- **Part 3 (Q1′ injectivity):** unaffected; the present construction gives, per record, a set of $(a,L)$ compatible with it, not uniqueness.
- **Dyadic notice:** as in part 1b (register the (D5) constants); nothing new.
