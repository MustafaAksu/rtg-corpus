### 3.1 The strongest new scientific opportunity: A1a without R9 on explicit \\(U\\)

[SESSION: derivation from owner-reported DYAD-THM-LPHASE-ROWA-REMAINDER and OWNER: RECOG v0.3.22 §6.13b]

The new Dyadic result states, on block starts in the explicit region \\(U\\),

\\[ D_M^U=\tau\sum\_{m=0}^{M-1}Q_m-\sum\_{m=0}^{M-1}r_m, \qquad |r_m|\le\frac{403}{250}Q_m^{3/2}. \\]

RECOG's existing reciprocal-amplitude theorem gives

\\[ Q_m^{-1}=m+Q_0^{-1}+O(\sqrt m). \\]

For sufficiently large \\(m\\), inversion therefore yields

\\[ Q_m=\frac1m+O(m^{-3/2}). \\]

Consequently both series

\\[ \sum\_{m\ge1}\left(Q_m-\frac1m\right), \qquad \sum\_{m\ge0}r_m \\]

converge absolutely. Since the harmonic sum differs from \\(\log M\\) by a convergent term,

\\[ \boxed{\lim\_{M\to\infty}(D_M^U-\tau\log M)\ \text{exists for every }x_0\in U.} \\]

This argument requires neither RECOG R9 nor NUMGEN INF-1. The previously proved finite-block identity and phase-transfer offset can then establish existence of the corresponding primitive and normalized asymptotic phase limits.

Important distinction: This is a [SESSION] proof candidate, not an adopted RECOG theorem or a retroactive upgrade of `RECOG-RESULT-A1A-PHASE-EXISTENCE-NG`. It would establish a stronger explicit-domain result with different dependencies. The existing result on \\(U\cap U\_{\rm NUMGEN}\\), including its provenance and grade, should remain intact.

Before promotion, I would require a concise proof cell checking the branch-lift convention, exact theorem domain, sum indexing, amplitude bounds, and convergence argument against adopted Dyadic v6.4.22. It should receive its own proposed RID rather than silently expanding the scope of the existing NUMGEN-based RID.
