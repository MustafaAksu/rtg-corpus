These are not literally the same interface.

Please choose one of two explicit dispositions:

- Quote the original RECOG W3 same-space prerequisite at source scope; **or**
- Label RRF's pre/post comparison as a **proposed extension**, not as RECOG W3 itself. This formula is a [SESSION] candidate interface, not an adopted physical transport map or RECOG theorem.
