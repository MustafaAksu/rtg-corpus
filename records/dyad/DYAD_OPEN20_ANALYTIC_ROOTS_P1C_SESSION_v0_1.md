## 1. P1-c-3 — 0 is an isolated fixed point

`[THM — SESSION]`; exact criterion, gate G2.

**Statement.**
- Let I ⊂ 𝒪 = ℂ{ρ, η} be the ideal generated by the two components of F − id. They are convergent, since T is entire.
- Then m⁷ ⊂ I. Consequently 0 is an **isolated** zero of F − id in ℂ², i.e. an isolated fixed point of the complexified F.
- dim_ℂ 𝒪/I = **13**.

**Proof.**
1. **Exact test.** For K = 3, …, 9 the script decides, by exact rational linear algebra in the primitive coordinates (ρ, η), whether m^K ⊂ I + m^{K+1}.
   - The test spans the products x^a y^b g_i with a + b ≤ K − 3, truncated at degree K, and checks every degree-K monomial for membership.
   - This condition depends only on the K-jet of F, which is exact here: T has rational Taylor coefficients.
   - Result: the condition **fails for K ≤ 6 and holds for K = 7, 8, 9**.
2. **Nakayama.** Apply Nakayama's lemma in the Noetherian local ring 𝒪 to the finitely generated module (m⁷ + I)/I. Since m·m⁷ = m⁸, the relation m⁷ ⊂ I + m⁸ gives m⁷ ⊂ I.
3. **Finite colength.** Hence 𝒪/I is finite-dimensional. By the Rückert Nullstellensatz, V(I) = {0} near 0.
4. **Multiplicity.** Because m⁷ ⊂ I, dim 𝒪/I = dim 𝒪/(I + m^{K+1}) for every K ≥ 6. The exact rank computation gives 13 for K = 6, …, 9. ∎

The jet computation is used here only as an *input* to Nakayama's lemma. The conclusion holds for the convergent germ, not just for a truncation, which is what the review required.

**Negative control M1.** Run on the cubic part of F − id alone, Q·(linear) with common factor Q = zw, the criterion fails for every K ≤ 9, as it must: that germ has a curve of zeros.

**Consequence** (`[THM — imported, hypotheses checked]`; original statement read only through Vivas's summary of Abate 2001).
- Abate (2001): a germ of (ℂ², 0) tangent to the identity with an **isolated** fixed point has at least ν − 1 parabolic curves, where ν is the order.
- The complexified F is holomorphic, tangent to the identity, has order 3, and by §1 has an isolated fixed point.
- So it has **at least two parabolic curves**.
- These are holomorphic discs with 0 on their **boundary**, not analytic curves through 0. They do **not** supply the convergent invariant curve Γ needed for the P1-b §3.4 criterion.

Checking Abate's original hypotheses line by line remains part of obligation P1-c-2.

## 2. P1-c-5 — formal invariant curves tangent to [1:0]

`[ALG]` / `[THM — SESSION]`; gates G3–G5.

**Equation.** Write the curve as w = φ(z) = Σ_{k≥2} a_k z^k. The invariance condition is E(z) := F_w(z, φ(z)) − φ(F_z(z, φ(z))) = 0.

**(a) Orders z², z³, z⁴** vanish identically, for every φ (G3). The linear parts cancel, and F_w(z, 0) has no term of degree below 5.

**(b) Order z⁵ fixes a₂ (forced, two values).** The coefficient of z⁵ is exactly quadratic in a₂ (G3):

  A a₂² + B a₂ + C = 0,  with
  A = μ̄ − 2μ = ½ + 2i√3,
  B = [F_w]_{(3,1)} − 2c₄₀ = 11/24 − 5i√3/24,
  C = c̄₀₅ = −61/2160 + 43i√3/6480 ≠ 0.

- C ≠ 0, so a₂ ≠ 0: w ≡ 0 is not invariant, and no invariant graph is flatter than z².
- The discriminant D = 1277/4320 + 281i√3/12960 is **not a square in ℚ(i√3)** (G4): its norm 1242787/13996800 is not a rational square.
- So the two roots a₂^± are conjugate over ℚ(i√3) and lie in the quadratic extension ℚ(i√3, √D). Numerically:
  - a₂^+ ≈ 0.0576581 − 0.0041559i;
  - a₂^− ≈ 0.0256752 + 0.1484934i.

**(c) Orders z^{k+3}, k ≥ 3: a_k forced, never free, never obstructed.**
- The coefficient of z^{k+3} is L_k·a_k + (terms in a₂, …, a_{k−1}). Coefficients a_m with m > k enter only at order ≥ m + 3, because the identity linear parts cancel at order m.
- Here

  L_k = [F_w]_{(3,1)} + 2(μ̄ − μ)a₂ − k(c₄₀ + μa₂) − 2μa₂ = ℓ·(k* − k),  ℓ := c₄₀ + μa₂.

- **Exact facts (G5):**
  - ℓ ≠ 0 for both roots, because the quadratic does not vanish at a₂ = −c₄₀/μ.
  - Reducing modulo the quadratic gives the **same** value for both roots: k* = −64/19 − (16√3/19)i.
  - Im k* ≠ 0, so L_k ≠ 0 for every integer k ≥ 3.
- **Control M2:** with the sign of the (μ̄ − μ) term flipped, no k* ∈ ℚ(i√3) satisfies the congruence, as required.
- **Conclusion:** at every degree k ≥ 3 the coefficient a_k is **forced** (uniquely determined). No degree is free and none is obstructed.
- G6, at 60 digits: the numerically obtained L_k agree with ℓ(k* − k) to below 10⁻⁵⁰ for k ≤ 13, and the residual of the computed curve is below 10⁻⁵⁰ through degree 16.

**(d) Statement.** `[THM — SESSION]`, exact.
- The complexified F has **exactly two** smooth formal invariant curves tangent to [1:0], namely w = φ_±(z) with φ_±(z) = a₂^± z² + …. Each is uniquely determined by a₂^±.
- Applying the real structure (z, w) ↦ (w̄, z̄), which commutes with F, gives exactly two smooth formal invariant curves tangent to [0:1].
- **Scope:** this concerns smooth curves written as graphs. Singular (Puiseux) formal branches tangent to [1:0] are excluded only by a leading-exponent balance, `[HEUR]`:
  - an exponent α < 2 would need α = [F_w]_{(3,1)}/c₄₀, which is not a real rational number;
  - an exponent α > 2 leaves the z⁵ term C ≠ 0 with nothing to cancel it.
  - A full Puiseux analysis is not done.

**(e) Restricted dynamics.** On each formal curve,

  F|_{Γ±} : z ↦ z + ℓ_± z⁴ + O(z⁵),  ℓ_± = c₄₀ + μa₂^±, ℓ₊ = −ℓ₋ ≈ ∓(0.0961279 − 0.0196970i) ≠ 0.

So F|_{Γ±} is a **non-trivial parabolic germ of multiplicity 4**, with 3 attracting and 3 repelling directions. The exact identity ℓ₊ + ℓ₋ = 2c₄₀ − μB/A = 0 is checked in gate G5.

## 3. What this gives for the B-an criterion of P1-b §3.4

- **Done:**
  - the fixed point is isolated (§1);
  - the candidate curves exist and are unique as formal objects (§2);
  - on them F restricts to a non-trivial parabolic germ (§2(e)).
- **Not done** (each is needed before criterion §3.4(c) can be applied):
  1. **Convergence of φ_±.** This is open.
     - `[NUM]` illustration, no inference: up to k = 21 the root test |a_k|^{1/k} rises slowly, from about 0.50 to 0.56 for φ₊ and from 0.57 to 0.63 for φ₋.
     - The same test on F itself gives about 0.92–0.98 (`curve_num_N24.json`).
     - These figures neither show convergence nor divergence. In this kind of setting formal invariant curves are often divergent (Gevrey), but that is a heuristic, `[HEUR]`.
  2. **If φ_± converges:** whether the half-iterate obstruction ½ ∉ 𝔅(F|_Γ) holds for the one-variable germ z + ℓz⁴ + …. This needs its formal invariant (résidu itératif) and its Écalle–Voronin data, computed for F|_Γ only.
  3. **If φ_± diverges:** the criterion is unavailable along this route. Two alternatives are recorded, both `[HEUR]`:
     - the parabolic curves of §1 together with sectorial normalization;
     - López-Hernanz–Raissy–Ribón–Sanz: parabolic curves asymptotic to formal invariant curves (arXiv:1411.2945; not yet read).
