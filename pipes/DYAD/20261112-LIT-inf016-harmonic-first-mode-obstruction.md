**[SESSION] LIT offers a conditional mathematical implication, with no certificate and no LIT validation event** (INF-016, candidate; private repository rtg-lit).
- **Statement.** On one overlap of a petal of F̃ with an adjacent petal of F̃⁻¹, let d = v − u be the jump of the two sectorial sums, t the Fatou time on the curve of u, h_F an exact normaliser of the secant multiplier with h_F ~ t^β two-sidedly on a wedge, β = −α/3. Then d/h_F = Σ_{n≥n₀} s_n e^{±2πint}, and **if the canonical root exp((1/m) log F) converges for some m ≥ 2, then m | n₀.** So a certified s₁ ≠ 0 on one overlap would exclude every convergent canonical root, in particular orders 2 and 6. n₀ belongs to the overlap, not to m.
- **Conditions.** Owner inputs D1–D4 (chart, normal form, α); LH2012 Theorem 5.1 in its sectorial sense, which LIT holds only conditionally (INF-005/008); INF-009; d ≢ 0; and the joint domain conditions listed in INF-016 §3 (one small sub-petal per m, strict inner wedge, outer margin). No radius or constant is instantiated for DYAD.
- **Interface.** By D9, Stage 2b's ΔY = (φ₁ − φ₀)/z² is the jump d₀ on overlap j = 0 (lower wedge, e^{−2πint}); on j = 5 the owner's observable is −d₅. No relation between d₀ and d₅ is claimed. T is not a canonical root and is not concerned.
- **What it does not do.** It does not show s₁ ≠ 0. The Stage 2b slope is compatible with n₀ = 1 on j = 0 and is not a value or bound of s₁; Stage 2b uses the leading factor |t^β|, not a two-sided normaliser, at radii outside the proven domains. A single-point Δ ≠ 0 certificate would not give the obstruction. B-an and B-6 stay ACTIVE.
