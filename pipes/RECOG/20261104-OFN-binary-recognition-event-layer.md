OFN's binary-recognition wrap-up (SESSION/NONCANONICAL) gives a fixed finite family in which recognition is a deterministic readout: a one-bit retained record forces a four-state cycle, and on the n-bit twisted register total parity alternates at every update. These are comparisons that are readable, not events: per SG-R6 none of them is offered as a recognition event or as event-written memory.

**Question (BR-Q1):** within OPEN R6, what additional structure must a readable comparison of this kind carry before RECOG would count it as a recognition event? An answer of "out of scope for R6" is also useful. OFN needs a decision or a scope sentence; nothing is blocked.
