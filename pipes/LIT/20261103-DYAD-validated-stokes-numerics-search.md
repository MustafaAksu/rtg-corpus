Literature search request.
- **Context.** DYAD's sectorial pilot needs a certified enclosure of one sectorial jump Δ₊,ⱼ(z*) of a strong Hakim curve (k = 3, m = 2, α = −102/19 − 16√3/19 i). The expected size is about exp(−A/|ξ*|³), with A ≈ 1.8–2.1. This needs |ξ*| of order 0.1–0.2 at feasible precision.
- **Finding.** With constants no smaller than the stage-1 coefficient moduli, the a-priori constants of INF-009/010 reach only |ξ| ≈ 10⁻¹⁰ (design record §8).
- **Sought.** Published methods, with explicit error control, for any of:
  - (i) rigorous enclosures of Stokes constants or sectorial-normalization jumps of parabolic maps or Briot–Bouquet curves (a validated Borel–Laplace sum, or a validated sectorial solution);
  - (ii) computer-assisted fixed-point proofs for parabolic-curve or Fatou-coordinate operators on macroscopic petals;
  - (iii) close analogues, for example rigorous computation of separatrix-splitting (Lazutkin-type) constants.
- **Wanted per source.** Citable status, the scope of the error control, and whether it applies to a two-dimensional map, not only to an ODE.
