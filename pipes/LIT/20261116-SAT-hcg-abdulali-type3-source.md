**Who and why.** HCG (satellite study, not RTG; PI authorisation of this request: 16 Nov 2026). HCG's OPEN 12 asks whether Severn's Corollary 12.1 (B. Severn, hexagon:2610.00387v2, §12; unrefereed) applies Abdulali's theorem correctly. Severn deduces the Hodge conjecture for all powers of certain abelian sixfolds of type III with K = ℚ(√−3) from his Theorem B and from **S. Abdulali, "Abelian varieties of type III and the Hodge conjecture", Internat. J. Math. 10 (1999), 667–675**, Theorem 4.1 (p. 673), which he applies to every member whose Hodge group is the generic one. HCG does not hold the paper.
**Request — literature support on this topic (not a verdict on one work):**
1. The source: Abdulali 1999, version-bound (`fulltext.md`), with Theorem 4.1, its proof, the definition of an *effective PEL-family of type III* and what "general" means in its §4 (Severn reads it as: a fibre whose Hodge group equals the generic one, rather than outside a countable union).
2. Anything later that corrects, extends or depends on that theorem (e.g. later work by Abdulali or others on type III, or on Weil cycles for a maximal subfield of the endomorphism algebra), and whether the discriminant of the restricted Weil-type structure is treated anywhere (Severn's Example 12.2; HCG recomputed its arithmetic only).
**What HCG needs back:** citable sources with scope notes and the rtg-lit commit, to `rtg-sat` `pipes/HCG/`; "nothing more found" on 2 is a useful answer. Nothing here is an RTG result or claims LIT-backed validation.
**2026-11-17 (receiver, LIT co-owners).** Answered in one item covering items 1 and 2: the 1999 paper itself was obtained (PI-supplied journal scan, stored privately, restricted). The discriminant literature was not read (coverage gap). `closed` means LIT has nothing further to do on this request; it is not HCG acceptance or adopted use, and does not close OPEN 12.
