## 0. Purpose and Epistemic Status of This Document

### 0.1 What This Document Is

OFN owns the programme's foundational commitments, hypotheses about primitive relational objects, and criteria for model selection. It asks why a distinction is physically meaningful, what an interaction event must account for, and which relational/gauge requirements constrain admissible dynamics. It does not derive the current continuous generator or the TDR-G rules by declaring them compatible.

`[DEF]` O0–O3 denotes conceptual commitments; O4 specified models; O5 their consequences. This is neither historical chronology nor the Legacy L0/L1/L2 or Programme-tier classification.

**v0.11 revision boundary:** owner synchronization with the editions released after v0.10 (Dyadic smooth realization, RRF class-C row, TDR-G Finding A acceptance and RID map, NUMGEN L-BACKBONE, the RECOG v0.3.20 freeze record, Triadic v1.6.1), two recorded definition corrections in the §5.5 contract (Standard C, Appendix B OFN11-C01; test-class regularity, OFN11-C02), six RID registrations for existing definition bodies (§§1.3, 4.1, 4.6, 8), and Methodology v1.4 tag spellings. The §5.5 contract binds a *named model class* and is not a universal axiom, a primacy verdict or a physical selection (SG-OFN-SELECTION, SG-OFN-PRIMACY).

### 0.5 Foundational Scope Guards

Programme SG-PROG-1–4 apply in their adopted v1.3 form (Programme v1.3 §8), with Methodology v1.4 §§3.3–3.4. The following local guards are adopted in v0.8; SG-ZN-GEOM remains in §4.6.

**[SG-OFN-EVIDENCE]** Do not promote an owner report, review deduction, historical note or completed archive task into physical or mathematical closure. Preserve source status separately from verification and unresolved contradictions.  
Violation: authority or evidence access is substituted for the actual proof/identification.

**[SG-OFN-STATE]** Do not replace a complete model state by phase differences, a scalar invariant, or a quotient unless the relevant observables and transitions are well-defined on that replacement.  
Violation: distinct allowed futures may be silently identified.

**[SG-OFN-PRESENTATION]** Distinguish passive relabeling, gauge changes, physical pole exchange and tangent half-turns. A chosen orientation convention is not a physical preference; full-map covariance must name its action and domain.  
Violation: a computational convention or a partial-row symmetry is asserted as physical invariance.

**[SG-OFN-SELECTION]** Adoption of a useful effective model is not generator selection; one failed route is not rejection of every route. Conditional technical results retain their stated grade while physical selection is open.  
Violation: model policy or proof dependency is mistaken for physical inevitability.

**[SG-OFN-PRIMACY]** Graph minimality, feedback, dynamical reduction and ontological priority are distinct. Neither a successful triad-to-dyad reduction nor its failure in a named class alone settles which object is physically primitive.  
Violation: a subsystem or counting result is promoted to an ontology verdict.

**[SG-OFN-RANK]** Keep affine/metric rank, mode count, graph cycle capacity, complex cohomology and physical curvature distinct. An additive scalar holonomy vector is not automatically a curvature rank.  
Violation: a shared number or rank name replaces the required representation and physical dictionary.

**[SG-OFN-TIME]** A formal update, section event, retained memory, state change and calibrated physical duration must not be identified without a rule connecting them. No single relational observable is declared the unique possible clock here.  
Violation: bookkeeping or an asymptotic inversion is treated as a complete time derivation.

**[SG-OFN-INFORMATION]** Do not infer a finite state set from finite description of each state, a mandatory antipodal event from an undefined orientation bit, or exact physical conservation from a failed statistical-model replacement.  
Violation: an unproved capacity, information or conservation premise becomes a hidden axiom.

## 1. Current Ontological Commitments (O0)

### 1.1 Relational Primacy

`[DEF]` [RID: RTG-OFN-DEF-RELATIONAL-CONTENT] **Physical content is relational.** An isolated node has no independently specified physical state in RTG. The programme begins with distinctions and relations; node-like carriers are meaningful through that relational structure. This commitment does not select a unique graph, generator or numerical coordinate.

`[DEF]` [RID: RTG-OFN-DEF-PHASE-GAUGE] **Global phase is gauge.** A common shift of all node phases leaves physical content unchanged in the declared phase models. The statement concerns absolute phase, not every state variable: advances/tokens and other variables needed by the model cannot be discarded merely because phase differences are gauge-invariant. The discrete and continuous state contracts are separated in §4.1.

### 1.2 Background Independence

`[DEF]` [RID: RTG-OFN-DEF-BACKGROUND-TIME] **No pre-given physical spacetime.** Physical time, spatial distance and a spacetime metric are not independent primitive backgrounds. Mathematical charts, cyclic order, graph distance and update indices used to specify a model are permitted, but their interpretation as physical time or geometry requires a separate construction. Relational events are candidate time-bearing structure; the event rule and duration calibration remain open.

`[DEF]` [RID: RTG-OFN-DEF-PRESENTATION-COVARIANCE] **No hidden referee.** A passive relabeling or admissible change of presentation must not alter a physical prediction. A deterministic update must transform covariantly; a stochastic rule would require covariance of its probability law. This is an admissibility requirement, not a claim that every current implementation has been certified against it.

### 1.3 The Zero-Origin Condition

`[DEF]` [RID: RTG-OFN-DEF-ZERO-ORIGIN] **Zero-origin.** $\mathcal O_0=0$ records the programme's null-origin convention. The first-dyad expression $0\to(A,B)$, $A+B=0$, is retained through the explicit Genesis definition reference in §11, IMP-O08-01. It is not a proof of a physical creation mechanism, binary-event inevitability, or exact conservation for every later interaction. IQP-E and the null-ledger conservation reading remain open in §9.

### 1.4 Positive Research Commitments and Their Limits

`[DEF]` The current requirements exclude externally imposed physical reference structure and label-dependent outcome selection from the adopted programme remit. They do not prohibit using force-like coordinates, formal counters, or mathematical representations. No broad physical momentum claim is rejected solely by calling it gauge: the source-specific symmetry and observable must be specified (§4.1).

`[HEUR]` The positive programme is to identify complete relational states, internally specified transitions, persistent distinctions and the conditions that create further relational structure. Geometry is sought through an explicit reconstruction; physical time through relational order and duration comparisons. The imported free-dyad law supplies one persistence witness (§3.3), not an interaction or creation law. Candidate capacity, orientation and primitive-object principles are tested against these construction tasks.

## 2. Primitive Relational Objects (O1)

> **[Local guard SG-OFN-PRIMACY]** Minimality depends on the declared object class; dynamical reduction and ontological selection are different claims.

### 2.1 The Dyad as Candidate Primitive

`[DEF]` In the adopted distinct-pole, simple-graph representation, $K_2$ is the minimal nonempty binary relation. This specifies that representation class; it is not a theorem excluding self-relations, richer arrows or a network-first ontology.

`[HEUR]` A dyad has no nontrivial simple undirected cycle. That does not mean every dyadic dynamical system lacks return, feedback, signed comparison or history. The sense in which an open relation could be physically incomplete is the live content of OPEN-OFN-3A.

### 2.2 The Triad as Candidate Primitive

`[HYP]` $K_3$ may be the minimal physically complete relational object. It is the first cycle in the simple undirected convention, but feedback loops, directed cycles and holonomy operators need their own definitions. Graph closure alone does not establish physical completeness.

`[HYP]` A minimal network may be the relevant primitive, with dyads and triads as subsystems. OPEN-OFN-3C asks for the physically justified size/connectivity criterion and geometric construction, not merely a node-count threshold. None of the three alternatives is selected in this revision.

## 3. Why Interaction Exists (O2)

### 3.1 The Central Claim: Interaction as Event, Not Force

`[HEUR]` Interaction is sought as a change triggered by internal relational conditions, not an externally imposed cause acting on pre-existing objects in physical background space. Rewriting a model in force-like coordinates does not violate this aim.

`[DEF]` The words *update*, *interaction event*, *birth*, *section crossing*, *comparison* and *retained memory* name different operations unless an owner supplies their identification. In particular, mathematical comparison or a nonzero return defect alone is not an event rule. OPEN-OFN-4 retains that formalization task; RECOG's request is recorded in §6.4, not accepted as a new ontology law.

### 3.2 Relational Distinguishability Pressure

`[HEUR]` Unresolved distinguishability motivates an interaction criterion. “Maximal distinction” does not by itself prove that continuation is impossible or fix an event threshold. The criterion must identify the comparator, state domain, conserved/accounted quantity and event response.

`[HEUR]` “Relational debt” is a candidate interpretation of a persistent distinction. No scalar debt functional, accumulation law, or discharge theorem is defined by that phrase here. A nonzero state remaining nonzero does not itself establish accumulation or a mandatory birth.
